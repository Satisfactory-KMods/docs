Once a plan exists, refine it until it matches the factory you want to build.

## Desired outputs

Open the **Outputs** panel to add or remove target items and change their
amounts. The whole plan recalculates automatically.

## Recipes & alternates

Each step shows the recipe it uses. Open **Summary → Plan validation → Recipe
routes** to inspect the available complete routes for each produced item. The
planner selects the best route for your current strategy automatically, but
you can choose from up to ten valid alternatives where they exist or switch
back to **Automatic (best route)**. Each option includes a short comparison of
route depth, raw-input score, and byproducts. You can also exclude recipes you
never want the planner to pick.

Under **Plan settings → Recipe Filters** you can exclude specific **raw
resources**, for example an ore you haven't unlocked mining for yet. Any
recipe that needs an excluded resource directly is skipped. The planner then
falls back to a different recipe or ore for that item where one exists. If no
route remains, it shows an error card instead of silently using the excluded
resource or a crushed variant that still depends on it.

The picker only contains genuine mineable/extractable resources. A normal
item is not added merely because the current catalog has no recipe producing
it.

The resolver only accepts a mineable resource or an item tagged
`KMods.PlannerResource` as a final external input. A normal item with no
producer is never silently treated as raw: calculation marks the plan invalid
and explains the unresolved item or cycle instead.

The recipe catalog is stored as a versioned item/recipe graph. Candidate
routes are only offered when their complete dependency chain reaches an
allowed raw resource or one of your Available Inputs. Calculating and balancing
those routes runs in a bounded backend worker pool, so a large plan does not
block unrelated API requests.

## Miners

Open **Configure Miner** from the Outputs panel and set the resource, node
**purity**, **miner tier**, and **overclock**. Not sure what overclock to
use? Enter a **target rate** instead and it calculates the overclock
percentage needed to hit it. Your configured miners show up under
**Configured Miners** in the same panel, and the calculator applies each one
directly wherever the plan needs that resource no manual available input
needed, and no fixed rate: the miner scales with the rest of the plan just
like a recipe node does, with its own output, byproduct, and any required
fluid input wired in as real connections.

A resource deposit is treated as the miner's extraction source, not as a
material that the miner consumes and reproduces. This matters for Fluid
Modules whose output is still the same resource: their required Water or other
fluid remains part of the material balance instead of being optimized away.
Waste Module replacement outputs and byproducts likewise remain real outputs
and can feed another recipe or appear as surplus.

For the Modular Miner, some resources also let you attach modules. The
**Waste Module** (Crusher or Smelter, pick one) replaces the raw ore with a
crushed intermediate or a primary ingot at the same total rate, plus a small
byproduct. The **Fluid Module** boosts the extraction rate using a supplied
fluid (Water, Muriatic Acid, etc.) some resources (Sulfur, for example) can
only be extracted with it attached at all. The two modules sit on
independent attachment slots in-game, so you can combine a Waste Module with
a Fluid Module on the same miner for both effects at once.

## Slug Hatchery

Open **Hatchery Info** from the Outputs panel and pick a slug color to see
the heat and humidity range it needs to hatch or breed, whether a Tank
module supplying a fluid is required, the Incubator tier, and which modules
to build. The mod treats hatching as something you test for in-game, so
these are target ranges to dial modules toward, not exact settings.

Any recipe elsewhere in your plan that consumes a slug directly also shows
that slug's conditions right on its own node, so you don't need to look it
up separately.

:::note
Purity comes from the actual node in the game world (impure/normal/pure) it's
not something you choose freely, so set it to match the node you actually plan
to build on.
:::

## Notes

Drop a sticky **note** anywhere to label parts of your factory. Double-click a
note to edit it, and recolor it if you like.

## Sub-assemblies

Select a few related blocks and right-click to **group** them into one tidy
block. You can collapse or expand this block, which keeps large plans
readable.

## Planning strategies

Under **Plan settings → Algorithm** you can control alternate recipes,
resource/building filters, and route priorities such as route depth, raw
resources, power, balance, or byproducts. Automatic selection ranks the
complete dependency route, not just the final recipe, and always remains
deterministic when scores tie. A manually selected route stays selected while
the other ranked options remain available. Identical recipe executions are
aggregated only after their complete material flows have been resolved. Some
advanced strategies are **Patreon** features. See the
[Patreon page](/production-planner/patreon) for details.
