No new features or fixes are planned for the planner. Work continues in the

The planner is free to use. Supporting kmods on **Patreon** unlocks higher
limits and the rest of the planning strategies. The **Patreon Bonuses** page in
the planner shows the same list with your own current usage.

## What a membership changes

- **Saved plans** How many production plans your account can keep.
  Free: 20 plans. Patreon: unlimited.
- **Snapshots** How many snapshots one plan can hold.
  Free: 3 per plan. Patreon: unlimited.
- **Recipe Strategy** How the planner decides which recipes to use.
  Free: Shortest Path and Balanced. Patreon: adds Shortest Chain, Fewest
  Resources, Low Power, and Byproduct Reuse. You can combine up to two either
  way.
- **Early access to mod updates** New mod features before they are released
  publicly.

Everything else in the planner is free, including the AWESOME Sink calculation,
the byproduct policies, suggesting a consuming recipe, auto-building a consumer
chain, and the weighted recipe routes described on the
[plans page](/production-planner/plans).

## Managing your bonus

Open the **Patreon Bonuses** page from the top bar to link or refresh your
Patreon membership. Once linked, your bonuses activate automatically across the
planner.

:::tip
Nothing you've already built is ever at risk. Bonuses only raise limits and
unlock extra strategies. Plans, snapshots and diagrams you made on the free tier
stay exactly as they are.
:::
