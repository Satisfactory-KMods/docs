The planner is free to use. Supporting kmods on **Patreon** unlocks higher
limits and pro planning features. The cards below show every bonus, your current
limits, and if you're a patron your live usage.

- **Saved Sessions** The number of production plans you can save and load.
  Free: 10 sessions. Patreon: 200 sessions.
- **Recipe Strategy** How the planner selects which recipes to use when calculating chains.
  Free: Balanced only. Patreon: All 5 strategies (Shortest Chain, Fewest Resources, Low Power, Byproduct Reuse).
- **Sink Byproducts** Automatically routes recipe byproducts to the AWESOME Sink and calculates the points/min generated.
  Free: Byproduct overview only. Patreon: Full sink calculation with pts/min.
- **Early Access to Mod Updates** Get early access to new mod features and updates before they are publicly released.
  Free: Standard release. Patreon: Early access to all updates.
- **Alt Recipe Selection** Choose which specific alternate recipes the planner may use instead of allowing all or none.
  Free: All alternate recipes or none. Patreon: Pick individual alternate recipes.
- **Weighted Multi-Recipe Paths** Force one item to use two production recipes and define each recipe's share of the produced output.
  Free: One forced recipe per item. Patreon: Mix two recipes with adjustable output percentages.
- **Tier Filter (Building Filter)** Exclude specific production buildings from the planner useful for restricting to early-game tiers.
  Free: All buildings enabled. Patreon: Exclude individual buildings.
- **[WIP] Export Graph (PNG / SVG)** Download your production graph as a high-resolution PNG image or a scalable SVG file.
  Free: Not available. Patreon: Export as PNG or SVG.
- **Suggest Consuming Recipe** Right-click an unused output to search for recipes that consume it, or auto-build a whole consumer chain several recipes deep.
  Free: Not available. Patreon: Suggest & auto-build consumer chains.

## Managing your bonus

Open the **Patreon Bonuses** page from the top bar to link or refresh your
Patreon membership. Once linked, your bonuses activate automatically across the
planner.

:::tip
Nothing you've already built is ever at risk. Bonuses only raise limits and
unlock extra actions plans, sessions and diagrams you made on the free tier
stay exactly as they are.
:::

## Suggest Consuming Recipe

This is one of the pro planning features. Right-click any unused output on
the canvas and search for a recipe that consumes it, or let the planner
auto-build a whole consumer chain several recipes deep in one click. Free
plans can still see that an output is unused, but they can't use this
shortcut to build a consumer for it.

## Weighted multi-recipe paths

Patrons can select up to two recipes for the same item in **Summary → Recipe
routes**. Set their output weights from 1–99%; changing either value adjusts the
other so the shares always total 100%. The planner enforces that mix in its
material balance, which can let one route consume a co-product created by the
other. Free plans keep the default of one recipe per item.
