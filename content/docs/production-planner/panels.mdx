The side panels turn your diagram into numbers you can act on. Click an icon
on the rail at the left edge of the sidebar to open that panel. Click the
same icon again to collapse it back down to just the rail. Your last-open
panel and whether it's open are remembered between visits. On desktop you
can also drag the panel's right edge to resize it; the width you pick is
remembered too. Press **1** to **7** to jump straight to a panel from
anywhere on the page.

## Outputs

Your target items and the amount per minute you asked for. A scale control
above the list multiplies every output quantity by a factor at once, handy
for scaling a whole plan up or down. Each output also has a fill button that
sets its quantity to a belt or pipeline tier's maximum throughput.
Fluid and gas fields use `m³/min` in the UI; the planner converts them to its
internal liter-based rates automatically (`1 m³ = 1,000` internal units).

## Balance

This panel compares target versus actual production for each output, so
you can spot shortfalls or overproduction at a glance. A summary line at
the top tells you at once whether everything's balanced. Unbalanced
outputs sort to the top.

## Byproducts

This panel lists the leftover items a recipe produces and shows which of
them can go to the AWESOME Sink. Everyone can see whether a plan has
sinkable byproducts at all, and with Patreon the planner also totals the
exact points per minute for you. Each card has a "focus on canvas" button
that jumps straight to that item on the diagram, plus an actions button
(⋮) with "Suggest Consuming Recipe" (Patreon) and "Add as Desired Output",
so you don't need to find the exact node on the diagram first.

Recipe outputs that aren't routed anywhere show up in their own "Unrouted
outputs" section below. Each of those rows has the same actions button,
plus "Route to Sink" (which the byproduct cards above skip, since they're
already routed to a sink). Duplicate catalog rows for the same output are
combined before the remainder is calculated, and numerical remnants that
would display as `0/min` or `0 m³/min` are omitted.

Panel headers and the canvas stay fixed when a list gets long. Only the active
panel content scrolls, and its scrollbar remains inside the sidebar instead of
making the whole planner page taller.

:::tip
Even on a free plan you can always see _whether_ a plan has sinkable
byproducts. Patreon only adds the exact points-per-minute total nothing is
hidden behind the perk, it's a convenience.
:::

## Stats

This panel gives you the detailed breakdown: total power draw, buildings
used, raw inputs, and recipe and connection counts, including
extraction/miner nodes. Long recipe lists get a search box and are grouped
by recipe category with collapsible sub-headings, and your
collapsed/expanded sections are remembered. Alternate recipes are tagged
**ALT**. When your plan draws power, the **Power Generation Assistant** lets
you pick which power plants to use, choose the fuel for plants with more
than one option, and see how many buildings and how much fuel are needed to
cover the draw.

### Pinned Recipes

Recipes you added with **Suggest Consuming Recipe** are listed here, under
Available Inputs. Each one is forced into the plan regardless of the route the
planner would otherwise pick, so this is where you set how much it should
produce and remove it again when you no longer want it. Editing the rate or
deleting an entry recalculates the plan straight away. The line under each name
shows how many buildings the current rate works out to.

## Summary

This panel gives you the big-picture totals for the whole plan: building
types and raw resources per minute, showing whether your configured miners
actually cover the demand. If any raw resource nodes have no miner
configured yet, an **Auto-configure miners** button sets a default
extraction rate for them. **Plan validation** also shows material-balance
errors, allowed external inputs, and the ranked recipe-route selector with up
to ten choices per active production step.

## Settings

Rename the plan, switch cloud saving on or off, and adjust the planning
options (strategy, alternates, overclocking, filters). **Rate decimals** controls
how many decimal places per-minute amounts show throughout the planner.
The two **Game Mode** settings can be selected when a plan is created and
changed later in its settings. **Recipe Parts Cost Multiplier** offers `0.25×`,
`0.5×`, `0.75×`, `1×`, `1.25×`, `1.5×`, `1.75×`, and `2×`. It multiplies every
per-craft ingredient amount used by recipes tagged `Recipe.Part`, then rounds
that ingredient to a whole number like the game. **Power Consumption
Multiplier** offers `0.25×`, `0.5×`, `0.75×`, `1×`, `2×`, and `5×` and applies
to the production power draw of every machine and configured miner. Changing
either setting recalculates the plan immediately.
Build-gun recipes and their building, vehicle, decoration, or customization
descriptors are excluded from target and recipe selectors because they are not
automated production routes. Items without any remaining allowed automated
producer are also omitted from target selectors, but remain available as manual
inputs.
