The Production Planner turns "I want X per minute" into a complete, buildable
factory plan. Every recipe, building, and raw resource is worked out for you
and drawn as a clear diagram. No spreadsheets, no math.

## Take the tour

The first time you open a plan, a guided tour walks you through the workspace:
naming the plan, choosing outputs, suggesting sub production lines, feeding in
material you already have, calculating, and reading the resulting graph. A
second tour explains every side panel one at a time, switching to each panel as
it goes.

Both tours can be replayed at any time from the two tour buttons in the
workspace toolbar (next to the **Guide** link); the first one runs itself only
once per browser. Press **Esc** or **Skip tour** to leave at any point.

## Create your first plan

Open the planner, click **New Plan**, and work through the five guided steps:

1. **Target** choose an item or a specific recipe. A recipe automatically uses
   its first output as the target and pins that recipe to it.
2. **Outputs** enter the amount per minute, add more desired outputs, and
   optionally force a final recipe for each output.
3. **Strategy** choose up to two planning priorities and decide whether the
   plan may use alternate recipes, overclocking, automatic sinking, and compact
   recipe groups.
4. **Availability** open the Recipe, Building, and Raw Resource catalogs to
   define what the solver may use. Enabled counts update immediately, while
   forced recipes remain protected from accidental disabling.
5. **Review** check outputs, strategy, availability, and any configuration
   warnings, then click **Create Plan**. The complete chain is calculated as
   the planner opens.

The step list scrolls horizontally on phones. On tablets and desktop it stays
visible vertically to the right of the active step, so you can see where you
are and return to an earlier completed step.

:::tip[Direct links]
The create page still accepts `itemId`, `recipeId`, `amount`, and
`forceEndRecipeId` query parameters. `recipeId` takes priority over `itemId`
and selects the recipe's first output; a valid `forceEndRecipeId` can override
the final recipe. Invalid values are shown as non-blocking hints so they can be
corrected in the wizard.
:::

:::tip
Not sure which recipe or page you need? Press **Ctrl+K** to search this whole
documentation instead of clicking through the sidebar.
:::

## What you'll see

You land on the **canvas**, where your factory is drawn as connected blocks.
Each block is one production step. The lines between them show how resources
flow, and the card-based side panels summarise build progress, power,
buildings, raw inputs, balance, snapshots, and more. You can also switch the
same calculation to a text checklist or expandable tree.
