The Production Planner turns "I want X per minute" into a complete, buildable
factory plan. Every recipe, building, and raw resource is worked out for you
and drawn as a clear diagram. No spreadsheets, no math.

## Create your first plan

1. Open the planner and click **New Plan**.
2. Choose what to make an **item** (e.g. _Reinforced Iron Plate_) or a specific
   **recipe**.
3. Enter the **amount per minute** you want.
4. Click **Create**. The full production chain is built instantly.

:::tip
Not sure which recipe or page you need? Press **Ctrl+K** to search this whole
documentation instead of clicking through the sidebar.
:::

## What you'll see

You land on the **canvas**, where your factory is drawn as connected blocks.
Each block is one production step. The lines between them show how resources
flow, and side panels summarise power, buildings, raw inputs, and more.
