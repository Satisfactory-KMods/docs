## 2.0-beta (July 2026)

- **Design refactor**: the planner home, session cards, creation flow, sidebar
  panels, and catalogs now share the same responsive card-based design. Phones
  use focused stacked layouts; tablet and desktop progressively add space for
  persistent navigation and side-by-side content.
- **Community Templates**: browse, search, and filter publicly shared plan
  templates on the new Templates page in the planner, then start a fresh plan
  seeded from one. Publish any of your own plans as a
  template (with a category) and unpublish it again later; a template's page
  has a copyable, deep-linkable share URL.
- **Create Plan redesigned** into one 5-step wizard for both item and recipe
  targets: Target → Outputs → Strategy → Availability → Review. The new
  **Availability** step restricts which Recipes, Buildings, and Raw Resources
  the plan may use with live enabled/total counts and real category/kind
  filters; the new **Review** step summarises everything with jump-back links
  and warns if a forced recipe depends on something you disabled elsewhere.
  Steps scroll horizontally on phones and sit vertically beside the card on
  tablet and desktop. Direct `itemId`, `recipeId`, `amount`, and
  `forceEndRecipeId` links remain supported.
- Recipe, Building, and Raw Resource catalogs replace long picker lists. They
  add search, real category/type and state filters, availability reasons,
  grouped collapsible sections, bulk actions, and compact/detailed recipe
  rows. Forced recipes are protected from accidental disabling.
- A new **Tree** view shows the calculated plan as an expandable hierarchy
  with rate/building/clock columns, resolved building images and wiki links,
  shared-node references, and CSV export. Switch between Flow, Text, and Tree
  from the workspace toolbar; your marked-built progress and the Calculate
  button work the same in every view.
- Surplus handling now offers **Preserve**, **Sink surplus**, and **Maximize
  Sink Points** objectives, plus a searchable item filter, per-item priority,
  and a rate to reserve instead of sinking. Point maximization stays inside
  the raw-resource budget of the requested factory instead of importing extra
  material just to score points.
- Each sunk item now gets its own AWESOME Sink building card and incoming
  amount instead of every material being merged into one node. Eligible
  surplus is routed through those cards; a catalog recipe with no real output
  is used when a fluid or gas must be disposed of rather than sunk.
- Fixed: ores, Oil, Air, and other extractor-backed resources now resolve
  through their real Modular Miner, Air Collector, or extractor recipe. The
  Text view's raw-resource total includes those extraction nodes as well as
  external inputs.
- Flow and Text now share role-specific cards: emerald raw inputs with drilling
  icons, amber bypasses with packages, purple outputs with checkmarks, and
  lime AWESOME Sinks with a money effect. A persistent topbar toggle controls
  all card animations and defaults to on.
- Recipe blocks on the canvas now get an animated highlight when overclocked
  (past 100%) using small lightning bolts that travel around the card,
  alongside the existing built (green) and Somersloop-boosted (violet)
  highlights; combined states stack, and the effect respects reduced-motion
  settings.
- Fixed: the active view and sidebar panel are now saved to the page URL, so
  a shared or reloaded link reopens the same view instead of always falling
  back to Flow.
- Fixed: switching to Text or Tree view and marking
  blocks as built could be lost on the next cloud reload; both are now saved
  and restored correctly.
- Removed the **Power Generation Assistant** from the Stats panel.

## 1.4-alpha (July 2026)

- Individual alternate-recipe selector, weighted two-recipe path selection, and building/tier filter are now free for every planner.
- PNG/SVG/PDF diagram export has been removed.
- Configure Miner now has **Power Modules**: a `+50%` and a `+25%` version, up to three of each at once. Their percentages add together like the Fluid Module's boost (three of each is `+225%`), and a Crusher or Smelter dampens the combined boost the same way it dampens a Fluid boost.
- Suggesting a consuming recipe now changes the actual plan. The picked recipe is pinned into the calculation, its other ingredients are produced for you, and its consumption counts in the material balance instead of only being drawn on the canvas.
- Configure Miner is reachable from the canvas: right-click a raw-resource node or an input row needing a mineable resource and pick **Configure Miner**.
- Configure Miner can copy a finished setup onto another resource: head tier, purity, overclock, and Power modules carry over, while Waste/Fluid modules are re-picked for the target resource.
- Picking an alternate recipe as a consumer now enables that one recipe for the plan, instead of failing because alternates are switched off. The global alternates setting is left alone.
- Deleting a node that came from a suggested consuming recipe also removes it from the plan, and a pinned recipe the planner can no longer use is dropped automatically instead of leaving the whole plan stuck on "no valid route".
- New **Pinned Recipes** list in the I/O panel: change how much a suggested consuming recipe should produce, see the building count it works out to, or remove it again. Previously such a recipe could only be added, never adjusted or deleted.
- Unused-output actions (suggest consumer, route to sink, add as desired output, highlight flow) now also work on raw-resource and byproduct nodes, not just recipe blocks.
- Miner overclock now tops out at `100%`, matching the real extractor.
- Fixed: a configured miner was scaled past the single physical building it represents. It is now capped at one unit, so demand it cannot cover is reported instead of silently satisfied.
- Fixed: the Balance panel compared against the wrong node, so every desired output showed `0/min` actual and a false "off target" warning even when the plan produced exactly the target.
- Fixed: right-clicking a byproduct node that had collected unrouted material opened no menu at all.
- Fixed: an unrouted byproduct from a configured miner could disappear from its node card entirely instead of showing as unused output.

## 1.3-alpha (July 2026)

- The canvas now zooms out to 10%, making even very large plans easier to see in full.
- Game Mode settings can be selected during plan creation or changed later: Recipe Parts Cost offers `0.25×` through `2×` with whole-ingredient rounding for `Recipe.Part`, while Power Consumption offers `0.25×` through `5×` for every production machine and configured miner.
- Fixed: the selected Recipe Parts Cost could be saved without rebuilding affected input rates; initial calculation and later graph rebuilds now use its rounded costs consistently.
- Fixed: build-gun recipes, building descriptors, and items without an allowed automated producer no longer appear in planner target or recipe selectors. Producerless items remain available as manual inputs.
- Fixed: fluid and gas amount fields now accept and display `m³/min`, convert to internal liters automatically, and no longer cap input at `99.999 m³/min`.

## 1.2-alpha (July 2026)

- The authoritative planner catalog is now a versioned item/recipe graph in MongoDB. Every selectable route must have a complete dependency path to an allowed raw resource or Available Input; unresolved items and cycles are reported instead of becoming implicit raw inputs.
- Heavy route scoring and material-balance calculations now run in a bounded backend worker pool. Large calculations no longer block unrelated API requests, and each worker reuses the current catalog snapshot.
- Plan validation now offers up to ten ranked recipe routes per active production step. The best complete route remains the automatic default, while each option shows concise depth, raw-input, and byproduct details and can be selected without losing the other choices.
- **Suggest sub production line**: pick a product in the Outputs panel and get a ranked catalog of small production lines that make it while eating your plan's current leftovers. Cards show machines, power, reused surplus per item, extra inputs and new byproducts; Preview solves a candidate with your plan for exact numbers, Add to plan merges it into one rebalanced plan. Free: 10 suggestions, 2 steps deep. Patreon: 50 suggestions, up to 6 steps.
- Fixed: a miner with an empty Fluid Module slot on a resource that doesn't need one no longer shows a Fluid Module among its components, and its power estimate no longer includes that phantom module.
- Fixed: Choose end recipe now only lists recipes the plan may actually use; recipes blocked by disabled recipes/buildings, savegame unlocks, or the alternate-recipe settings are no longer offered, and the action is hidden when only one route remains.
- Fixed: a failed savegame import now shows the real reason (unsupported version, corrupt file, service unreachable) instead of an internal error message, and an import that matches no planner recipe no longer locks every recipe.
- Fixed: a configured Fluid Module whose miner outputs the same resource no longer gets optimized out. Its required fluid is now a real material input, while Waste Module replacement outputs and byproducts remain real outputs/surplus. Older saved miner configurations receive compatible fallbacks.
- Fixed: Unrouted Outputs no longer lists duplicate output rows or numerical remnants displayed as `0/min` or `0 m³/min`.
- Fixed: long sidebar panels now scroll their content internally instead of expanding the whole planner page; the panel header, footer, and canvas remain fixed.
- Fixed: Exclude Raw Resources now only offers genuine mineable/extractable resources; producerless normal or catalog-only items no longer appear in the picker.
- Fixed: configured raw-resource nodes now show the required whole miner/extractor building count in their header, always rounded up from fractional machine demand.
- Fixed: recipes locked to 100% clock now use whole building counts in backend material balancing. Boiler/heater inputs and outputs run at their full per-building rates, upstream supply grows to match, and excess output becomes surplus.
- Fixed: unsuffixed per-minute rates above 100 now keep their configured decimal precision throughout the planner.
- Fixed: Low Power now scores the actual producing-building consumption across the complete route. Disabled producers are removed before scoring, and the selected enabled building is preserved in the canvas, statistics, and exports.
- Fixed: combined strategies are blended evenly regardless of selection order, while Byproduct Reuse now prefers inputs that are genuinely produced as co-products elsewhere in the active plan.
- Fixed: planner-resource-tagged items are valid external inputs, circular routes are rejected before efficiency ranking, and malformed configured-miner values now produce a safe validation error instead of invalid flow numbers.
- Fixed: building counts and power totals now use physical clock-distributed buildings consistently in Stats, the page header, and exports. Variable-power recipes, configured-miner scaling, and Somersloop output are included without applying their multipliers twice.
- Fixed: manual supply/sink actions now reuse the backend graph's hub IDs, and clearing all desired outputs also clears stale plan diagnostics.
- Fixed: a manually inserted consumer recipe with no routed output edge now shows its theoretical output rate instead of `0/min`; the actually routed amount remains separate for unused-output and trim actions.
- Premium strategies and automatic byproduct sinking are now enforced by the build API as well as the planner UI.

## 1.1-alpha (July 2026)

- Configured miners are now real production nodes. A configured miner no longer needs a matching Available Input the calculator applies it directly wherever the plan needs its resource, scaling with the rest of the plan and wiring its output, byproduct, and any required fluid input as real connections (proper input/output handles, clock/power display), instead of a fixed-rate external input.
- A Configured Miners list (Outputs panel) shows what's set up; click an entry to edit its settings, or remove it.
- The Modular Miner's Waste Module (Crusher/Smelter) and Fluid Module can now be combined on the same miner, matching the real building's two independent attachment slots. Configure Miner shows them as two separate pickers instead of one shared module choice.
- The miner resource picker now only lists resources with real module data, so you can't pick one the game data has nothing to configure for.
- Tooltips added to icon-only buttons across the planner (fill tier, remove, configure) so their purpose is clear on hover.
- Fixed: excluding a raw resource now forces route selection to avoid it; when no valid alternative exists, the planner shows an error card instead of using the excluded resource or its crushed variant.
- Fixed: Byproduct Reuse no longer creates a circular byproduct route that produces and sinks a co-product while using that same co-product's byproduct as an input.
- Fixed: recipe selection now falls back to another complete input chain when the preferred route cannot be supplied, and shows an error when none can.
- Fixed: production chains now stay on the selected output's dependency routes; unrelated co-product recipes no longer inflate a small plan into millions of raw resources, and every external input must be a real raw resource.
- Fixed: saved per-plan alternate-recipe selections are now normalized and preserved when loading, importing, restoring snapshots, or creating a plan from a template.

## 1.0-alpha (July 2026)

- Modular Miner modules in Configure Miner: **Crusher**/**Smelter** swap the ore output for a crushed intermediate or ingot plus a small waste byproduct; **Fluid** boosts extraction with a supplied fluid and is required for some resources (e.g. Sulfur). Module outputs, byproducts, and fluid inputs are wired into the real plan graph.
- Recipes can be tagged to never overclock; they now stay pinned at exactly 100% clock everywhere no overclock, no underclock, with extra demand rounding up to a whole additional building.
- Hatchery Info button next to Configure Miner: pick a slug color to see its heat/humidity ranges, required Tank fluid, Incubator tier, hatch/breeding durations, power draw, and needed modules.
- Byproduct Reuse strategy (Patreon, Plan settings → Algorithm): prefers recipes that consume a byproduct already produced elsewhere in the plan; anything it can't cover is filled the normal way.
- Combine up to 2 strategies at once: pairing Byproduct Reuse with another strategy tries the byproduct gate first, then falls back; any other pair blends both scores evenly.
- Fixed: the Fluid module's boost combined with node purity multiplicatively instead of additively like in-game; miner output and required fluid now match real numbers on any purity.
- Fixed: Byproduct Reuse could promise supply that was never actually produced (a hub showing 0/min produced but routing a real amount out); such shortfalls are now backfilled from a fresh source.
- Fixed: recipes without a single shared cadence (Fluid Conditioner bypass outputs, Slug Hatchery/Terrarium timers) showed 0 rates on the canvas, in panels, and in exports; they now fall back to each item's own per-minute rate.
