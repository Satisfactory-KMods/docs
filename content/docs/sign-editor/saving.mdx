## Saving

**Save** is at the end of the action bar. A tick beside it means everything is
stored; it disappears the moment you change something.

Free accounts can keep **20** designs. Patreon supporters have no limit.

## Unsaved drafts

Your work in progress is kept in **this browser** as you go. Reload the page, or
come back after a crash, and the editor restores what you had rather than the
last saved version.

When that happens you get an **Unsaved draft** badge in the action bar. Next to
it is a discard button that throws the draft away and reloads the saved design.

:::note
Drafts are per design and per browser. They are not synced to your account, so
they will not follow you to another machine **Save** is still what makes a
change permanent.
:::

## Public designs and sharing

The **Public / Private** toggle in the left panel decides who can open your
design.

- **Private** only you.
- **Public** anyone with the link can open a read-only preview.

Once a public design is saved, two buttons appear in the action bar: one copies
the **share link**, the other opens the **preview page** yourself. The preview
is read-only, works on phones, and lets visitors search the sign's elements.

## Export and import

**Export** shows the design as JSON, ready to copy to the clipboard or download
as a file.

:::caution
If the **arrow material** is enabled, the export contains **no elements** the
mod does not draw them under that material. The dialog says how many were left
out before you copy anything.
:::

**Import** takes the same JSON back, either pasted in or dropped in as a `.json`
file.

<Steps>

1. Click **Import** in the action bar.

2. Paste the JSON, or drop a `.json` file onto the upload area.

3. The design is validated before anything is replaced if it does not match the
   sign schema, you get the errors and your current design is left alone.

</Steps>

:::note
Importing replaces the design you currently have open. Save first if you want to
keep it.
:::
