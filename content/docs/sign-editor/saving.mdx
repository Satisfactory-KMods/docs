## Saving

**Save** is at the end of the [editor's action bar](/sign-editor/). A tick
beside it means everything is stored; it disappears the moment you change
something.

Free accounts can keep **20** designs. Patreon supporters have no limit.

## Unsaved drafts

The editor keeps your work in progress in **this browser**. After a reload or
crash, it restores that draft instead of the last saved version.

When that happens you get an **Unsaved draft** badge in the action bar. Next to
it is a discard button that throws the draft away and reloads the saved design.

:::note
Drafts belong to one design in one browser and do not sync to your account.
They will not follow you to another machine. Use **Save** to make a change
permanent.
:::

## Public designs and sharing

The **Public / Private** toggle in the left panel decides who can open your
design.

- **Private:** only you can open the design.
- **Public:** anyone with the link can open a read-only preview.

Once a public design is saved, two buttons appear in the action bar: one copies
the **share link**, the other opens the **preview page** yourself. The preview
is read-only, works on phones, and lets visitors search the sign's elements.

## Export and import

**Export** shows the design as JSON, ready to copy to the clipboard or download
as a file.

:::caution
If the [**arrow material**](/sign-editor/sign-settings/#arrow-material) is
enabled, the export contains **no elements** because the mod does not draw
them under that material. Before you copy anything, the dialog reports how
many elements it omitted.
:::

**Import** takes the same JSON back, either pasted in or dropped in as a `.json`
file.

<Steps>

1. Click **Import** in the action bar.

2. Paste the JSON, or drop a `.json` file onto the upload area.

3. The editor validates the design before replacing anything. If it does not
   match the sign schema, the editor shows the errors and leaves your current
   design alone.

</Steps>

:::note
Importing replaces the design you currently have open. Save first if you want to
keep it.
:::
