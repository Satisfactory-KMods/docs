Choose every texture through the **Icon & image browser**. Open it with
**Add element → Image**, or from the inspector when an image or effect element
is selected. The [elements guide](/sign-editor/elements/#the-three-types)
explains the settings available after choosing one.

## The three tabs

| Tab                    | What is in it                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Game & mod icons**   | Item, building and schematic icons extracted from the installed mods.             |
| **RSS sign images**    | Arrows, symbols, plates and other sign textures shipped with Refined Sign Systems. |
| **My Images**          | Images you added yourself, by URL.                                                |

## Finding an image

Search matches **names, tags and descriptions**, so a rough word usually finds
the right thing even when the exact name is different.

Two filters narrow it further:

- **Mod:** which mod the asset came from, by its real name (for example
  *Fluid Extras*, not `ab_fluidextras`).
- **Type:** the kind of asset, such as an item, building or schematic.

Each card shows the mod and type it belongs to. Where a wiki entry exists, a
small book icon in the corner opens the k-mods wiki for it.

## Using one

Click a card to use it. If you opened the browser from **Add element → Image**,
it creates a new element; if you opened it from a selected element, it swaps
that element's texture and leaves its position and size alone.

You can also **drag a card straight onto the [canvas](/sign-editor/canvas/)** to drop the image exactly
where you want it.

## My Images

Anything the built-in catalog does not cover, you add yourself.

<Steps>

1. Open the **My Images** tab and click **Add**.

2. Paste a direct **image URL** and give it a name.

3. Save. It is now available in every design on your account.

</Steps>

:::caution
The mod downloads your image **in game, from that URL**. The link must remain
publicly reachable. Images will not render from a private host, an expired
link, or a page that returns HTML instead of an image file.
:::

Free accounts can keep **50** custom images. Patreon supporters have no limit.
