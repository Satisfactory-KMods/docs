These apply to the whole sign rather than one element. They live at the bottom
of the right-hand inspector and stay available even while an element is
selected.

## Size

The sign size decides the pixel canvas the mod renders, from `256×256` up to
`2500×200`. It is the first thing you pick, and everything else stays locked
until you do.

:::caution
Changing size after you have placed elements does **not** move them. They keep
their pixel positions, so some may end up outside the new canvas. Anything
hanging over the edge is drawn faded in the editor and cropped in game.
:::

## Sign type

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Normal">

A flat, lit sign. This is the default and the one that supports the parallax and
arrow materials below.

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Hologram">

Renders translucent, with scanlines across the whole face. It adds its own
controls:

| Setting                 | What it does                                     |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Distortion intensity**| How much the image wobbles.                      |
| **Scanline intensity**  | How hard the scanlines read.                     |
| **Border intensity**    | The glow around the sign's edge.                 |
| **Use scanlines**       | Turns the lines off while keeping the glow.      |

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Material

| Setting                | What it does                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Background color**   | The sign's base colour. Also tints the parallax grid and the arrow material.  |
| **Emissive intensity** | How brightly the sign glows. Higher is brighter.                             |
| **Panner speed X / Y** | Scrolls the whole sign's material. **Negative values scroll the other way.** |

## Parallax & arrows

Two extra materials, available on **Normal** signs only.

### Parallax

Draws a receding depth grid behind your elements, speckled with small readouts
that tick over as you watch. Tinted by the background colour.

### Arrow material

Fills the sign with large scrolling chevrons, tinted by the background colour,
with its own **direction** and **speed**.

:::danger
The arrow material covers the entire sign face, so the mod draws **no elements**
underneath it. The editor hides them to match, and they are left out of exported
files — the export dialog tells you how many were dropped.
:::

### Scale

**Parallax / arrow scale X** and **Y** size the grid cells and the chevron
bands. `-1` means "use the mod's own default ratio", not a scale of one.

:::note
A hologram ignores both effects. If you switch a sign that had them enabled over
to hologram, the toggles disappear and nothing of them is previewed or exported —
the mod would ignore them anyway.
:::
