The Sign Editor is a browser-based designer for **Refined Sign Systems (RSS)**
signs. Arrange [text, icons and effects](/sign-editor/elements/) on a real-size
[canvas](/sign-editor/canvas/), [save the design](/sign-editor/saving/) to
your account, and load it in game without editing JSON by hand.

## Build your first sign

<Steps>

1. Open the editor and click **New design**. It opens with an empty canvas.

2. Give it a **name** in the left panel. This is what you will recognise it by
   in your design list.

3. Pick a [**sign size**](/sign-editor/sign-settings/#size). You must choose it
   before the remaining settings unlock because it defines the pixel canvas
   rendered by the mod.

4. Add an [element](/sign-editor/elements/) with **Text**, **Image** or **Effect** above the layer list.

5. Drag it into place on the canvas, and adjust it in the right-hand panel.

6. Click **Save**. The design is stored on your account, ready to load in game.

</Steps>

:::tip
The action bar has two guided tours. The **?** button covers the workspace; the
**shape** button explains elements and the image browser.
:::

## The workspace

The editor uses three columns:

- **Left:** the design's name, whether it is public, the sign size, the
  **Add element** buttons, and the layer list.
- **Middle:** the action bar and the canvas your sign is drawn on.
- **Right:** the inspector, with settings for the selected element and below them
  the settings for the sign as a whole.

:::caution
The editor needs a mouse and a wide screen, so it is not available on phones.
The read-only preview page works on any device.
:::
