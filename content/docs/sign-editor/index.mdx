The Sign Editor is a browser-based designer for **Refined Sign Systems (RSS)**
signs. Lay out text, icons and effects on a real-size canvas, save the design to
your account, and load it in game — no editing JSON by hand.

## Build your first sign

<Steps>

1. Open the editor and click **New design**. It opens with an empty canvas.

2. Give it a **name** in the left panel. This is what you will recognise it by
   in your design list.

3. Pick a **sign size**. This is the one setting you have to choose before
   anything else unlocks — it decides the pixel canvas the mod renders.

4. Add an element with **Text**, **Image** or **Effect** above the layer list.

5. Drag it into place on the canvas, and adjust it in the right-hand panel.

6. Click **Save**. The design is stored on your account, ready to load in game.

</Steps>

:::tip
Two guided tours are built into the editor, on the **?** and **shape** buttons
in the action bar: one walks through the workspace, the other covers elements
and the image browser.
:::

## The workspace

The editor is three columns:

- **Left** — the design's name, whether it is public, the sign size, the
  **Add element** buttons, and the layer list.
- **Middle** — the action bar and the canvas your sign is drawn on.
- **Right** — the inspector: settings for the selected element, and below them
  the settings for the sign as a whole.

:::caution
The editor needs a mouse and a wide screen, so it is not available on phones.
The read-only preview page works on any device.
:::
