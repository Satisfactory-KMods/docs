These guides assume you have completed [Create your first pack](/kdataforge/getting-started). Each
guide starts from a goal, provides the required files and YAML, and ends with a result you can check.
Use [Authoring concepts and document types](/kdataforge/features) when you need to understand a
capability, or [Reference](/kdataforge/reference) when you need an exact field or default.

## Create content

- [Create an item, icon, recipe, and Tier 1 unlock](/kdataforge/tutorials/item-icon-recipe-schematic)
  builds a common first content chain and makes it obtainable in a new save.
- [Create a research tree](/kdataforge/tutorials/research-trees) generates the tree, places MAM
  schematics in its node graph, and shows how to extend or prune its branches.
- [Add a recipe to an existing schematic](/kdataforge/tutorials/existing-schematic-unlock) adds an
  unlock to a vanilla schematic, another mod's schematic, or one from an earlier pack version.

## Integrate and balance

- [Balance content by tag](/kdataforge/tutorials/tag-based-bulk-balancing) registers a gameplay tag
  and edits every matching target with one patch.
- [Build a conditional multi-mod pack](/kdataforge/tutorials/conditional-multi-mod-packs) adds
  content only when a required mod version or class is present, without a hard dependency.
- [Edit another mod's data assets](/kdataforge/tutorials/editing-another-mods-data-assets) patches
  existing KAPI or RefinedR&D `PrimaryDataAsset` instances without generating replacements.

## Prepare and maintain a release

- [Localize your pack](/kdataforge/tutorials/localizing-your-pack) adds translated text and safely
  overrides existing vanilla or mod strings.
- [Rename generated content safely](/kdataforge/tutorials/renaming-content-safely) separates file
  renames from identity changes and shows where `redirects:` can preserve saves.
- [Turn live edits into a shareable pack](/kdataforge/tutorials/from-live-edits-to-a-shareable-pack)
  exports changes from `/kdf editor` and verifies that they survive a restart.

## Diagnose a failure

- [Debug a pack that will not apply](/kdataforge/tutorials/debugging-a-pack-that-wont-apply) checks
  discovery, parsing, conditions, targets, operations, reload limits, and logs in order.

For complete packs that combine several document types, continue to [Examples](/kdataforge/examples).
