The [K-Mods home page](https://k-mods.com) brings together the wiki, Production
Modeler and RSS Sign Builder. Its mod collection shows public mods reported
compatible with the stable game version on FICSIT.app. Compatibility notes stay
visible beside each mod; check those notes before installing.

In the [SatisfactoryPlus wiki](https://k-mods.com/wiki), start with a search or
choose the recipes, buildings or items catalog. Catalogs open as cards on your
first visit and remember your choice between cards and tables. Progression,
research, the resource map and comparison tools are available below the main
catalogs and in the sidebar. Sign in to save entries and write guides.

## Production Modeler

Draw a factory and calculate its flows, or ask the Modeler to plan a chain from
"I want X per minute" and edit the result. The guide covers the canvas, special
buildings, automatic planning and saving.

[Read the Production Modeler guide](/production-modeler)

:::tip
Press **Ctrl+K** (or **/**) anywhere in the docs to search. Use the **arrow
keys** to move between results and **Enter** to open one.
:::
