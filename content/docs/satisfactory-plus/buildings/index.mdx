## Reading these guides

Exact recipes belong in the in-game production menu because SF+ updates and
compatibility packs can change them. Building guides focus on setup, connections,
controls, constraints, and reasons a machine may stop.

A **dependency integration** uses a building supplied by another required mod,
such as Refined Power, Ficsit Farming, or Fluid Extras. An **upcoming** section
is not part of the released build yet.
