Satisfactory Plus uses three power models. Conventional generators hold fixed output while required inputs remain
available. Modular plants pass heat, working fluid, RPM, and power through attached buildings. Renewable generators
derive output from time, placement, nearby machines, or a claimed world node.

Find costs, unlocks, accepted-item lists, and throughput values in in-game wiki.

## Conventional generators

### Fixed-output state

The Coal-Powered Generator is released content. It produces **200 MW** at normal clock speed and accepts several
supported solid fuels. The fuel type determines burn time through its energy value. Water is a separate required input.
The generator stops when either the fuel or Water buffer can no longer support the next production step.

The coal generator aims to hold the output selected by its clock speed. Low grid demand does not turn it into a
demand-following generator. A stable belt supply is insufficient when Water flow fluctuates, and a full pipe is
insufficient when the upstream system cannot replace ongoing use.

The Fuel-Powered Generator follows the same fixed-output model with one compatible fluid or gas fuel in the pipe
network. SF+ changes its rating and progression, but does not give it modular heater or turbine stages. Flush mixed
pipes before changing the fuel because incompatible residue can prevent a valid fuel state.

The Nuclear Power Plant also uses the fixed-output model. SF+ requires Cooling Water and keeps nuclear waste as a
blocking output. A valid fuel rod, coolant supply, and waste capacity must all remain available. Failure of any one
condition stops production.

The Alien Power Augmenter keeps the base-game power mechanic. SF+ extends fuel acceptance and progression without
routing it through the modular Refined Power chain.

## Power Storage

Power Storage charges from a surplus on the connected grid. When consumption exceeds live production, the grid draws
stored energy. SF+ changes storage and charge limits while keeping discharge uncapped by its balance patch.

Storage sees only its connected circuit. A separate subgrid cannot charge from a surplus elsewhere. Empty storage
provides no startup reserve even when the power connection is valid.

## Modular Refined Power chain

Refined Power is a required SF+ dependency. A modular plant passes state through attachments:

```text
fuel -> heater -> heat -> boiler <- working fluid
          |                   |
          v                   v
      byproduct        steam or energized fluid
          |                   |
          v                   v
    vent or process       turbine -> RPM -> generator -> grid
                              |
                              v
                     spent fluid or condensate
```

The heater and boiler share a Boiler Platform. The standard turbine and generator share a Converter Platform. The
super-tier pair uses a Super Conductor Platform. The vent stack attaches to a Gas Vent Platform. Buildings placed nearby
without a valid attachment do not exchange heat or RPM.

### Operating-mode selection

Each module chooses an operating mode from the supplied item or fluid. That mode defines the valid input, output, cycle
time, heat, turbine behavior, or generator curve.

Selection follows gameplay state:

1. The input buffer receives an item or fluid.
2. The machine checks which mode accepts that content.
3. The matching mode becomes active.
4. The machine checks its attachment, pause state, required input, and output capacity.
5. The completed cycle consumes the profile input and stores the profile output.

Changing fluids can leave old content in the internal buffer. Until it is flushed or consumed, the machine may keep the
previous mode or refuse a new one.

### Heater state

The heater stores its current fuel and heat. Solid heaters pull fuel from the belt inventory. Solution heaters pull
fluid or gas through a pipe. A valid cycle consumes the selected fuel, adds heat, and stores the configured byproduct.

Fuel use is independent of boiler progress. The heater can continue burning while the boiler is blocked or empty, so a
downstream shutdown can waste fuel.

The boiler must attach to the heater through the same platform. Adjacent buildings on separate platforms cannot share
heat.

### Boiler state

The boiler reads heat from the attached heater and selects an operating mode from the working fluid. Its production
curve converts available heat into input consumption and output rate. A hotter heater changes both values. Each cycle
removes working fluid, consumes heat, and stores output fluid.

The boiler stops when the heater attachment is invalid, no mode accepts the supplied fluid, heat is insufficient, the
input runs dry, or the output buffer is full. Water-to-Steam and Slug Slime modes use the same mechanic. The wiki holds
concrete rates because values can change without changing the building behavior.

### Turbine, RPM, and generator state

The turbine selects an operating mode from the incoming working fluid. It consumes input, stores the configured output,
and changes RPM over time. Missing input or a blocked output lets RPM fall.

The attached generator converts RPM through its power curve. A larger generator reaches its nominal output only when a
compatible turbine and fluid supply maintain the required RPM. A turbine and generator on different platforms do not
share rotation.

The higher-tier steam turbine covers the RPM range required by high-voltage generation. The super-tier turbine and
generator follow the same chain on a Super Conductor Platform with their own profiles.

### Backpressure

A blocked final output propagates through the complete plant:

1. Turbine cannot store spent fluid or condensate.
2. Turbine stops consuming working fluid and loses RPM.
3. Generator output falls.
4. Boiler output fills and stops using heat.
5. Heater may continue consuming fuel until its own byproduct output fills.

Trace a stalled plant from the final output toward the heater. Increasing fuel or Water does not solve a downstream
blockage.

### Venting

Steam and gas stacks remove supported content without recoverable output. They still select operating mode from buffered
input. Unsupported fluid, mixed pipe, or incompatible residue prevents operation.

Flush the stack before changing the gas. Use overflow routing when the same byproduct also feeds production, since the
stack destroys everything it accepts.

## Solar panels

Each Refined Power solar type produces by day, night, or both. A day-state change switches the panel between daytime
output, nighttime output, and zero. Some models rotate visually toward the sun, but rotation itself is not a production
requirement.

The shared day state updates every panel together. Output still belongs to each panel's own grid connection.

### Upcoming placement limits in 1.4

The next major release adds a separate world-wide cap for each solar type. The placement preview recounts existing
buildings of the same type. Below the cap, the warning is informational. At the cap, construction is blocked. Setting
the cap to zero disables it.

This cap logic ships with 1.4.

## Solar Power Tower and Mirrors

A Solar Power Mirror links to one tower. It turns toward the tower target and tests for a clear line of sight. Only
linked, unobstructed mirrors contribute heat.

Mirror heat follows daytime progress. The tower sums the heat from valid mirrors and clamps the result to the supported
range. It then selects a conversion profile from the actual fluid in its input buffer.

Production needs at least one linked mirror, a valid profile, enough total heat, sufficient input, and room in the
output. A blocked output halts conversion. An obstacle between the mirror and tower lowers heat and can drop the plant
below the profile threshold.

Copying the tower settings preserves the tower link, letting the same link be pasted to several mirrors. Reassigning a
mirror removes it from the previous tower before linking the new one.

## Wind turbines

Refined Power calculates wind output from the turbine's height curve. Dynamic wind mode also scales the result with the
current wind state. The result is divided by the configured local turbine-capacity value, then the power multiplier is
applied. The nearby-turbine count stops production when the local maximum is reached.

This local spacing rule is current gameplay. A turbine can be validly placed and later stop when the neighborhood
reaches the limit.

### Upcoming placement limits in 1.4

The next major release adds a separate world-wide cap for each wind turbine class. It only decides whether another
turbine may be built. The local Refined Power rule still decides output after placement.

Distant dismantling may free a global build slot without improving local wind. Moving nearby turbines may improve local
wind without freeing a global slot. The world-wide cap ships with 1.4.

## Water Turbine

The Water Turbine claims a dedicated Water Turbine Node and marks it occupied. Dismantling the turbine releases the
node. A visible river or lake without a node is invalid.

SF+ sets the base output to **60 MW** while the turbine owns a valid node and is not paused. Refined Power then applies
the turbine maximum and configured multiplier. An invalid or already occupied node prevents a valid production state.

## Water Dam

The Water Dam claims a Water Dam Node. The node provides the dam endpoints, section count, and base production. The
section count controls the dam width, total output, Water amount per cycle, and dismantle refund.

The power calculation is:

```text
node production x section count x configured multiplier
```

Power Only mode uses the full result. Water and Power mode halves the output and enables Water production. Before a
cycle, the dam checks whether its internal Water buffer can accept the section-scaled amount. A full buffer stops
production. The output pipe drains stored Water; the UI flush empties the buffer.

The dam also requires a valid node and grid connection. Dismantling releases the site. The Water Dam already exists.
Release 1.4 keeps the existing building and changes its progression and settings.

## Release boundary

The Coal-Powered Generator and the current mechanics above are released.

Upcoming 1.4 adds a configurable power-production multiplier, world-wide solar and wind placement caps, and revised
Water Dam progression. These settings, full multiplayer support, and other 1.4 progression changes remain pending until
the release ships.

## Diagnose by state

For a conventional generator, check the fuel state, required fluid, and any blocking output such as nuclear waste.

For a modular plant, work backward: generator attachment and RPM, turbine output room and input fluid, boiler output
room and heater attachment, then heater fuel and byproduct room.

For a renewable building, separate production from placement. Solar depends on the time state. Wind depends on height,
wind state, and local density. Upcoming SF+ caps affect construction only. Water buildings require a valid unoccupied
node.
