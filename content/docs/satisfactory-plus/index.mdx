Satisfactory Plus (SF+) is a large content mod. To stay maintainable alongside the rest of the k-mods ecosystem (and to let other mods extend or rebalance it without needing SF+'s own source), it plugs into two different k-mods tools, at two different levels of coupling:

- **KAPI** is a **hard dependency**, compiled and linked directly into SF+'s C++. Used to _create_ the mod's own data-driven content (new miners, cleaners, air collectors, ...) as real `UPrimaryDataAsset` instances that any other KAPI-aware mod can discover and read.
- **KDataForge** is a **soft dependency**, resolved at runtime via Unreal reflection with no build-time link at all. Used by _third-party pack authors_ to patch or extend SF+'s (and any other mod's) KAPI data assets from plain YAML, without writing any C++.
