Use the panel beside the [canvas](/production-modeler/canvas/) to switch between three tabs:

- **Summary:** factory totals and requirements.
- **Inspector:** causes, repair previews, optimization hints and node settings.
- **Sub-models:** what this model offers to other models, and the models placed in it.

- **Desktop:** select one node to open Inspector, reopening a closed panel.
- **Mobile:** selection alone does not open the panel; use **Edit** or **Show summary**.
- Expand the mobile panel for more height, then restore it to see the canvas.
- Resize the desktop panel by dragging its inner edge or focusing it and pressing left/right.
- Close the panel for more canvas space; **Show summary** reopens it.


## Summary

1. Choose a section kind, then press **Add section**.
2. Set its filters to answer your question.
3. Add another section to compare views, including two of the same kind.
4. Collapse a section to hide details, or close it to remove it.

- Sections, filters, scope and panel width are kept with the saved model.

- **Search summary** filters rows by name.
- **Copy summary** copies the current scope and filtered results as text.

- Check **Components** and **Solved** before relying on totals from an incomplete calculation.
- Empty sections can mean no matches or missing results, not zero requirements.

For compact totals with precise tooltips, adjust [Number formats](/production-modeler/settings/#number-formats).

### Scope

- **Everything:** the whole model.
- **Current container:** nodes directly inside the container you are viewing.
- **Current container and below:** also includes nested contents.
- **Selected:** only selected nodes.
- **Selected and connected:** follows connections through the chain in both directions.
- **Selected and below:** also includes contents of selected containers.

Scope applies to added sections, belts and pipes, and unlocks.
**Mods** always describes the whole model.


### What a section can count

- **Output:** starts with Unused, including open outputs and surplus left after connected flows.
- Add Used, Sunk, Storage or Depot to include output sent to those destinations.
- **Input:** starts with Unmade, the inputs with no incoming connection.
- Add Made, Mined / extracted or Taken from storage to include connected supplies.
- **Output sink points:** AWESOME Sink points per minute from flows sent to sinks.
- **Input sink points:** what the filtered inputs would be worth if sunk instead.
- **Overclock:** Power Shards and Somersloops used.
- **Machines:** building counts, whole buildings and idle capacity where relevant.
- Building limits show remaining allowance or excess when the catalog supplies them.
- **Costs:** construction materials for calculated buildings in scope.

**Power** reports:

- Average net power and minimum production minus maximum use.
- Average and maximum use.
- Minimum and average production before and after boosting.
- Production boost percentage.

Production and net figures can show **N/A** for incomplete results or a partial connected scope.
Try **Selected and connected** or **Everything**, then check Inspector.
N/A does not mean zero production.

These sections appear automatically when data is available:

- **Belts and pipes:** lines needed for solved connections inside the scope, plus the busiest line's load.
- **Unlocks needed:** required milestones and research.
- **Mods:** sources used by the model.

Click a linked part or machine name to select matching nodes and reveal the first match.
This also changes selection-based scopes.

## Inspector

Inspector combines **Causes**, **Optimizations** and **Settings**. Without a
selection, Causes shows the whole model. Selecting a related node narrows the
findings; **Show the whole model** restores the broader view. A finding identifies
the input or constraint responsible for a blocked chain and the production
targets it affects. **Copy report** copies the analysis as readable text.

**Details** opens a modeless view. The canvas stays interactive. Clicking a node
or port reference reveals it and docks the details beside the canvas without
closing the diagnosis. Expand the view for more room. Catalog icons, port
labels and tooltips identify what each reference points to. Proposed nodes are
marked **New** and can be inspected, but appear on the canvas only after Apply.
The detailed mathematical evidence stays under **Why this is proven**.

**Find repairs** groups compatible existing sources separately from new supply.
Each option identifies the output it uses and how many nodes and connections it
adds. Compatibility alone does not establish capacity. **Preview repair** checks
the full proposed model before showing its result. A unique small local change
can instead offer **Check and apply if safe**. It applies immediately only when
the checked baseline and proposed result meet that safety condition.

The result shows the actual connection, source and destination. **Current model**
and **With this repair** switch the connection view without editing anything.
Required buildings, retained target settings, achieved rates and proposed
surpluses are separate sections. A single new extractor node can represent
several physical buildings. Hover a value for its more precise reading.

An unsolved current group has no usable numerical baseline. Its current flows
and building counts read **Unknown**, not zero. Surplus rows describe totals in
the proposed model; they are not automatically new waste caused by the repair.
Partial results identify the remaining problem. Incomplete or blocked checks
cannot be applied as a completed repair. A model change invalidates checked
results, and locking a cause removes its repair actions.

**Apply change** creates one undo step for the entire repair. **Apply partial
repair** explicitly applies a proposal that solves the affected group while
other groups remain unresolved. For larger alternatives, **Start Auto-Chain at
this input** opens the existing planner and returns its proposal for checking.
Changing a production target stays a separate, explicit alternative with a
preview. It changes the requirement instead of fixing its original supply.

Open inputs that the model treats as external supply are valid. Their local
extractor and chain actions are optional. **Optimizations** keeps surplus and
local improvement hints separate from blocking causes. A required co-product
is not automatically waste.

Select one node and open **Settings** for detailed controls.
**Custom title** changes its canvas name without changing its catalog entry.

Recipes offer clock, Somersloops and capacity.
These settings share one card; the collapsible input and output ports sit outside it.
Other controls depend on the node, as covered in [Special buildings](/production-modeler/special-buildings/).

A fixed recipe count sets the downstream plan in Basic and Full. If a connected
input cannot supply that count, Inspector reports the missing amount while the
downstream values remain visible. The recipe input displays its required rate;
the connection displays the available supply. Add that supply to make the plan
achievable. Manual mode continues to require balanced exact values.

For quick adjustments, use the clock input and clickable Somersloop pill in the
node's bottom section on the [canvas](/production-modeler/canvas/#node-status-and-quick-controls).
They remain visible when recipe details are collapsed. A locked node can still
be inspected, but must be unlocked before editing.

- Select at least two clockable machines to get a shared clock control.
- At least two eligible recipes with slots also get a shared Somersloop control.
- Bulk clock and Somersloop changes keep each node's fixed building count, upper
  bound or Auto setting. They change only the chosen modifier and use one undo step.
- Net power appears when available.

Once the plan is solved, the Inspector also lists the Power Shards and
Somersloops the node needs for all its buildings, or the sum over a
multi-selection. Each building above 100% clock takes one Power Shard per
started 50%, so 101 to 150% take one, 151 to 200% two and 201 to 250% three.
A building the plan uses only in part runs at that part of the clock: 2.5
buildings at 250% are two at 250% and one at 125%, seven shards in all.
Somersloops are the node's slots times all its buildings, the partly used one
included. Hover a total to see the sum node by node.
Nothing appears when the node needs neither.

Expand **Input Ports** or **Output Ports** to edit a port:

1. Drag its handle or use the arrows to reorder it within its direction.
2. Add, remove or change parts only where the node supports editable ports.
3. Enter a nonnegative limit in the displayed per-minute unit.
4. Choose **At most** for a ceiling, **At least** for a minimum or **Exactly** for a
   required rate.
5. Clear the limit to remove it.

A limit on an unconnected port sizes the node.
Move connections earlier or later to set priority in **Full** mode.
An output with several connections gives each one a **share** field: the
percentage of that output the connection takes. Leave it empty for Auto.
Click an endpoint name to reveal the other node.
Connection controls add or remove waypoints and disconnect one or all connections.
**Align to connection** moves the node vertically, using the first connection.

Inspector edits apply directly and support Undo; keep them with the normal [saving controls](/production-modeler/saving/).

## Sub-models

The first block reads this model's own interface off the current calculation:
every part its **Model Input** nodes take in and its **Model Output** nodes
hand out, with the rate one copy of the model moves, and the power one copy
draws and makes. That is what another model receives when it places this one.
Interface nodes without a part or a settled flow are counted separately.

The second block lists the models placed in this one with the copies the
calculation found, their power and revision. Click a name to reveal the node,
open the model it stands for, or refresh one or all of them. See
[reusing a model](/production-modeler/canvas/#reusing-a-model-in-another-model).

## Messages

Calculation messages and failed operations are shown inside Inspector. Proven
causes appear before their affected results, and remaining solver diagnostics
stay available in Causes. Counts identify blocking findings; **Copy report**
keeps the explanation and its references together. An empty selected scope is
not proof that the whole model is solved, so check **Show the whole model** and
the current calculation status.

Selected-data, missing-data and read-only notices stay in the canvas column without pushing down the sidebar.
A long-running calculation shows **Stop**, which cancels it without changing the model.

If usable data is missing, editing and calculation pause.
Use the header's **Game data** control and the [game-data guide](/production-modeler/game-data/) to recover.
Unsaved edits remain protected by the leave warning during this temporary lock.
A failed **Save and close** keeps the model open.
Choose **Keep editing** to resolve the problem; **Leave anyway** discards unsaved work.
Saving device preferences in [Settings](/production-modeler/settings/) does not save a locked model.
