Hi Pioneers! A big one this time. Your factories can now be folded up and
dropped into other factories, outposts and blueprints have been merged into one
Existing Factory building that reports its own power draw, the survey team has
finished mapping the whole planet and draws every belt and rail at its real
width, Refined Power water dam nodes show up, deposits get their own drawer,
the caves are open for business, and the solver has been reminded that an idle
building is a compliance violation.

ADA: "A factory inside a factory is not recursion, Pioneer. It is efficiency."

## NEW FEATURES

- **Sub-models: one factory as a building in another.** Place **Model Input**
  and **Model Output** nodes to declare what a model takes in and hands out,
  then pick **Sub-model** in another plan's palette and choose it from your
  models, the ones on this device or the public ones. It lands as one node that
  scales like a recipe: ask for twice its output and the calculation counts two
  copies, inputs and power included. Whole copies by default, a copy limit as
  ceiling or exact count, no overclock or Somersloops (FICSIT does not
  overclock whole factories), lockable and markable as built. The new
  **Sub-models** tab shows what your model offers and the models placed in it.
  Open the model it stands for in a new tab, or in the same window with a way
  back on the desktop; placed models are brought up to date when the plan
  opens and on request.
- **Existing factories count themselves.** Leave a factory empty and set a
  rate on each of its ports, on the canvas or in the Inspector: that is what
  one factory moves. The count you draw is where the plan starts; the
  calculation grows it when it needs more of them and leaves it alone when
  nothing pulls on it. With machines inside, the same rates are limits on the
  boundary and the count is yours.
- **Special nodes explain themselves.** Hover a special node in the palette
  and it tells you what it is, how it is used and why it exists, so the sink,
  the depot or a model input no longer have to be built to be understood.
  ADA approves of reading the manual.
- **One Existing Factory building.** The outpost and the blueprint are now a
  single **Existing Factory** container. It keeps the boundary ports and the
  count, and adds a title, an icon picked from the item and building images,
  and a stated power: enter the MW the site consumes or generates and the
  summary counts it with the rest of the model. Inputs always sit ahead of
  outputs; a new input slots in before them. Saved models open unchanged,
  including SFMD imports and models shared by link; only their port order
  moves inputs first.
- **The map knows the planet now.** Crash sites, roads, spawn points, the
  world border, gas pillars, spore flowers and the large and small rocks you
  can blow up are built into the wiki map as their own layers. Every crash
  site tells you what it costs to crack open (the item and count, or the power
  in MW); with a save loaded it also says whether that hard drive is already
  yours. Rocks, pillars and spore flowers show what a loaded save has already
  cleared. Everything works offline, no MAM uplink required.
- **Vehicles and trains on the map.** A loaded save now places your trucks,
  tractors, explorers, factory carts, drones, locomotives and freight wagons
  as their own world-object group. Yes, even the cart.
- **Water dam nodes on the map.** Refined Power's water dam nodes now show up
  with the line the dam is built along, the number of sections and the output
  in MW at that spot. A loaded save marks the ones that already carry a dam or
  a turbine as taken. Needs a fresh export with the updated exporter.

## QUALITY OF LIFE

- **A tidier map panel.** The filter panel is wider, pushes the map aside
  instead of covering it, and lists everything as compact rows instead of
  tiles. Buildings from a save are grouped by their build-menu category and
  sub-category with fold-out headings; the caves, crash sites and other world
  layers share one Map layers section.
- **Belts, pipes, rails and roads drawn to scale.** Lines on the map are now
  as wide as the real thing (a belt 1.35 m, a rail 6 m, a road corridor
  25 m) and grow as you zoom in instead of staying hairlines. Power lines are
  drawn between the poles and machines they connect, and hovering any line
  shows its length.
- **Deposits get their own loot tab.** Hand-mined deposits now live in a
  Deposits section on the wiki map, apart from the resource nodes you park a
  miner on. Every resource remembers its own on/off switch and shows up by
  default. On the map they are hexagons, so they never pass for a node.
- **Caves are open for exploration.** The underground caves and their
  entrances are on the map from the start. Spelunking over? The Underground
  section switches them off.

## BUGFIXES

- **A way out of an open factory.** The canvas shows a Leave button while a
  container is open, so touch screens and a focused button no longer trap you
  inside; a container deleted while open is left automatically. No Pioneer
  left behind.
- **The wiki window opens on Windows.** Opening the wiki beside the modeler
  left a white window that could not be closed, and the modeler with it: the
  window was built inside a synchronous native command, which deadlocks
  Windows. It is built asynchronously now, and the wiki window no longer asks
  for the update checks only the main window may run.
- **What a factory delivers to its boundary leaves the model.** A machine
  inside an existing factory that hands its product to a boundary port with
  nothing wired outside was forced to zero, so a pinned "to outside" rate
  reported that the group could only reach 0. The product now leaves the
  model like any unused output, and a rate pinned on a boundary port that is
  wired on both sides reads once across the boundary instead of splitting
  into half in and half out.
- **Buildings that lost their name.** A saved building whose class the wiki
  keeps under a different folder or a mod name (Pipeline Mk.2, the Seed
  Extractor), or that only has a build-menu entry, now shows its real name,
  icon and category instead of a raw class identifier. The few classes the
  wiki data does not know at all get a readable name and a small question
  mark in the panel instead of vanishing.
- **Slugs spotted.** The blue, yellow and purple power-slug icons show on the
  desktop wiki map again. Markers and the World objects legend use the slug
  item images from the active game-data pack.
- **No more idle machines at the end of the line.** A dead-end auto building
  takes everything its supply delivers. A recipe behind an auto-count miner,
  or behind a storage fed by several producers, now scales to the full
  upstream flow instead of stopping at one building and complaining about a
  surplus.
- **Pin your Modular Miner output.** Set an exact rate or an upper limit on a
  merged Modular Miner output without breaking the plan. The port keeps its
  rate, the miner count follows the pin, and returning the port to automatic
  restores it.
- **Overclocked generators stop cheating.** A generator placed as a burn
  recipe (Coal-Powered, Fuel or Nuclear) now makes power linearly with its
  clock. It used to borrow the power-consumption curve, so 22 generators at
  250% claimed more MW than the 55 they replace at 100%. FICSIT auditors have
  been notified.
- **Dev links go to dev.** Models shared from the dev site and the dev desktop
  channel link to dev.k-mods.com instead of production.
- **A drawn supply is a plan.** With two auto-count miners feeding one chain,
  the calculation used to hold both at their drawn counts and leave the
  larger supply half unused. It now uses that supply whole and grows the
  other miner to match, so 240 crushed ore no longer sits behind one SAM node.
- **Numbers, not formulas.** Every amount reads as a decimal: 217.5, never
  435/2. That covers warnings, tooltips, the fields you edit and the arrow-key
  steps in them. The Fraction number style is gone from the settings.
