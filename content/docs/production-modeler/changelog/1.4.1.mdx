## QUALITY OF LIFE

- **Close the offline notice.** The desktop notice about being offline or the
  server status being unavailable now has a close button. It stays closed until
  the app restarts.
- **Choose how often to check for updates.** Settings has an **Updates**
  section in the desktop app. Check every minute, every 15 minutes, every hour,
  every 6 hours, once a day, or only when the app starts.
- **Fullscreen.** The canvas toolbar has a fullscreen button, also on **F11**.
  It fills the screen with the canvas and hides the model header and the build
  bar. Press it again to bring them back.
- **Larger text when zoomed out.** A new Appearance setting, off by default.
  While you are zoomed far out, each card shows only its name and main figure in
  text large enough to read. The cards keep their size, so nothing moves.

## BUGFIXES

- **Connected byproduct recycling.** A multi-output recipe can feed an unused
  byproduct into a connected recipe and send its surplus to a Sink without
  reducing the flow needed elsewhere. Dark Matter Residue and Crystals are one
  example.
- **Space Elevator deliveries.** A configured exact input rate now works as a
  delivery demand even when the catalog has no phase cost or timing data.
  Connecting an elevator at that rate no longer invalidates its production chain.
- **Bundled game data updates.** Desktop picks up changed Wiki and image data
  even when the catalog version stays the same. Imported overrides remain
  untouched until you restore the bundled profile.
