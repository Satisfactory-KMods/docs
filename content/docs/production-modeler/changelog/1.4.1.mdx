## Quality of life

- **Offline notice closable.** Offline and server-status notice on desktop has
  a close button; stays closed until restart.
- **Update check interval.** **Settings**, **Updates** (desktop): every minute,
  15 minutes, hour, 6 hours, once a day, or only at start.
- **Fullscreen.** Canvas toolbar button or **F11**; hides the model header and
  the build bar.
- **Larger text when zoomed out.** **Settings**, **Appearance**, off by
  default: far out, cards show only name and main figure in readable text. Card
  size unchanged.

## Bug fixes

- **Byproduct recycling.** Unused byproduct can feed a connected recipe, surplus
  to a Sink, without cutting flow elsewhere (e.g. Dark Matter Residue,
  Crystals).
- **Space Elevator deliveries.** Exact input rate counts as delivery demand
  without phase cost or timing data; chain stays valid.
- **Bundled game data updates.** Desktop picks up changed Wiki and image data
  with an unchanged catalog version. Imported overrides untouched until the
  bundled profile is restored.
