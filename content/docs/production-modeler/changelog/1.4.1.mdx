## BUGFIXES

- **Connected byproduct recycling.** A multi-output recipe can feed an unused
  byproduct into a connected recipe and send its surplus to a Sink without
  reducing the flow needed elsewhere. Dark Matter Residue and Crystals are one
  example.
- **Space Elevator deliveries.** A configured exact input rate now works as a
  delivery demand even when the catalog has no phase cost or timing data.
  Connecting an elevator at that rate no longer invalidates its production chain.
- **Bundled game data updates.** Desktop picks up changed Wiki and image data
  even when the catalog version stays the same. Imported overrides remain
  untouched until you restore the bundled profile.
