## NEW FEATURES

- **Plan several targets at once.** The Auto Planner takes more than one
  target: on the review step, **Add another target** keeps the current one and
  starts the next. Chains that need the same intermediates or resources share
  one production step and one input. A target that another chain also
  consumes gets its own producer, so its full rate still leaves as product.
- **Add a node: categories.** The left column lists categories read from the
  game data: raw resources, liquids, gases, fuel, nuclear, organic, farming,
  packaging, Space Elevator parts, alternate recipes, power, and each
  producing machine. Mod packs sort the same way. Counts follow the search.
- **Add a node: details.** One click shows the entry at the bottom of the
  dialog: inputs and outputs per minute, how many recipes make or use each
  part, the machines with their power, cycle time, the milestone or research
  that unlocks it, and a link to its wiki page. Double click or Enter adds it.
- **Connection menu.** Right-click a connection label or line, or press the
  menu key or Shift+F10 on a focused label: select the source, the target or
  the whole chain, serve earlier or later at a split or merge, reset the
  label, delete. The menu takes the focus and the arrow keys move through it.
  Read-only models offer the selection entries only.
- **Older desktop versions.** The download page lists earlier builds and
  releases under **Older versions**, each with its platform downloads. When
  the storage for a site is full, the oldest version is deleted automatically.
- **Home page tabs.** Datapacks, Your Models and Public Models on one sliding
  switch. The header names the open tab, each tab has its own address
  (`/production-modeler/datapacks`, `/models`, `/public`) and the last one
  comes back. `/production-modeler/browse` leads to Public Models.
- **Model library as a file manager.** A sidebar that browses folders and
  models, a details list or tiles of the open folder, and a details pane.
  Double-click or Enter opens, Backspace goes up, F2 renames a folder, Delete
  deletes. New folders are named in place, folders drag onto other folders
  with everything inside, and a folder menu offers Open, New subfolder,
  Rename, Move to and Delete.
  - **Model library** at the top of the sidebar counts every model and opens
    the top level: folders first, then the models in no folder. Each folder
    lists its subfolders, then its models.
  - A click on a model in the sidebar opens its folder with the model
    selected; a double click opens the model.
  - Models and folders also drag from the sidebar. Dropping on
    **Model library** or the first crumb of the address bar moves them to the
    top level.
  - The details list is the default view. Tiles show what a model produces
    and needs, its nodes, its last change and where it is stored. Folders
    always come first, in the tiles as their own band above the models.
- **Model details pane.** What a model produces and needs, its interface with
  the rates set on it, the buildings it uses, node, connection, recipe,
  factory and sub-model counts, folder, tags, revision, dates, and whether it
  uses the current game data. Cloud models load on selection. Public models
  offer Open, Copy to my models and Copy link.

## QUALITY OF LIFE

- **Device settings from the home page.** Opens machine defaults, saves,
  numbers and appearance (updates and data on desktop) without a model.
- **Leaving with unsaved changes.** **Settings**, **Saves**: ask every time
  (default), save automatically, or discard. A failed automatic save still
  asks.
- **Click a connection label to select it.** Dragging the label still moves
  it.
- **Modular Miners merged in plans.** The Auto Planner always places a
  Modular Miner with both outputs on one belt.
- **Favorites in Add a node.** Star an entry; favorites get their own row at
  the top, kept per device.
- **Follow a part.** Click an input or output in the details to list the
  recipes that make or use it, with their counts. **Back** restores the search.
- **Search prefixes.** `in:coal` or `out:steel ingot` searches only inputs or
  outputs; a prefix covers the words after it, so `in:coal out:steel ingot`
  finds what turns coal into Steel Ingots.
- **Home page layout.** The header no longer links to Create and the
  Production Planner. The library header holds the address bar, the search,
  the storage switch, the sort, the view switch and the tag chips, next to
  **New model**, **Import file** and **New folder**. The stat tiles and the
  public teaser are gone; counts are in the library status bar.

## BUGFIXES

- **Whole-machine rounding removed.** The option "Round the solved machine
  count up to whole machines" is gone. It rounded counts up inside the solve
  and broke whole calculations. Counts stay exact. Models and SFMD files that
  still carry the option load as before and ignore it.
- **Even split behind exact values.** A split now serves exact rates first,
  also ones fixed several nodes further down the chain. It then shares the
  rest evenly between the Auto chains, at every split point. One chain no
  longer takes everything while the others get 0. A changed connection order
  still applies the priority.
