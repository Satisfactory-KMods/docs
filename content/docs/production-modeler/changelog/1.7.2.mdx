## NEW FEATURES

- **Change a node’s recipe.** Select a recipe node and choose another recipe in
  the Inspector. Recipes matching existing connections appear first, followed
  by all other supported recipes. Compatible connections stay attached;
  incompatible connections are removed. Undo restores the previous recipe.

## BUGFIXES

- **Zoom rendering.** The zoom readout updates independently, avoiding
  unnecessary redraws of other canvas controls.
- **Grid at distant zoom.** The grid hides when its cells are too small to
  distinguish and returns when zooming in. Snapping is unchanged.
- **Connections stay visible.** Canvas connections and their context menus render
  again after the Vue Flow update.
