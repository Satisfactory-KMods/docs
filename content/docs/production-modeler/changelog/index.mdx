What's new in the Modeler. Newest first.
These are app versions, not game-data versions. Each version has its own page
in the **Changelog** category.

## 1.4.0

The wiki map learns the whole planet (crash sites with their cost, roads, spawn points, world border, rocks, gas pillars and spore flowers), draws belts, pipes, rails and power lines to scale, shows Refined Power water dam nodes, groups saved buildings by build-menu category in a wider panel, separates deposits, opens the caves by default, names every saved building, and the solver stops leaving buildings idle.

[Read the 1.4.0 release notes](/production-modeler/changelog/1.4.0/).

## 1.3.0

Desktop wiki support, direct port-rate editing, model icons and colours, one snapping grid with Ctrl to override, BurnerManufacturer, Solar Tower and Icarus planning, larger browser packs, smoother canvas interaction, and corrected outpost supply and draft recovery.

[Read the 1.3.0 release notes](/production-modeler/changelog/1.3.0/).

## 1.2.1

Reliable Windows game-data imports, unlimited desktop packs, a 128 MiB browser limit, and complete icon exports.

[Read the 1.2.1 release notes](/production-modeler/changelog/1.2.1/).

## 1.2.0

Bundled Vanilla on desktop, version details, clearer update controls, and cloud saving for older or local game data.

[Read the 1.2.0 release notes](/production-modeler/changelog/1.2.0/).

## 1.1.0

Game-data switching, construction tracking, and canvas improvements.

[Read the 1.1.0 release notes](/production-modeler/changelog/1.1.0/).

## 1.0.0

The original factory canvas, calculations, storage, and desktop baseline.

[Read the 1.0.0 release notes](/production-modeler/changelog/1.0.0/).
