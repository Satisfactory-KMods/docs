Patch notes from the FICSIT R&D floor, newest first. These are app versions,
not game-data versions. Each version has its own page in the **Changelog**
category.

## 1.4.0

Factories fold into other factories as sub-model nodes that scale like recipes, outposts and blueprints become one Existing Factory building with stated power and its own icon, every special node explains itself in the palette. The wiki map has surveyed the whole planet (crash sites with their cost, roads, spawn points, world border, rocks, gas pillars and spore flowers), draws belts, pipes, rails and power lines at real width, shows Refined Power water dam nodes, groups saved buildings by build-menu category, separates deposits, opens the caves, names every saved building, and the solver no longer leaves buildings idle.

[Read the 1.4.0 patch notes](/production-modeler/changelog/1.4.0/).

## 1.3.0

The wiki moves into the desktop app, ports take a rate on the canvas, models get icons and colours, one snapping grid with Ctrl to override, BurnerManufacturer, Solar Tower, Icarus and Modular Reactor planning, larger browser packs, smoother canvas interaction, and corrected outpost supply and draft recovery.

[Read the 1.3.0 patch notes](/production-modeler/changelog/1.3.0/).

## 1.2.1

[Read the 1.2.1 patch notes](/production-modeler/changelog/1.2.1/).
