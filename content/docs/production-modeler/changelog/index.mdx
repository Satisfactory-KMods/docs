Patch notes, newest first. App versions, not game-data versions. Each version
has its own page in the **Changelog** category.

## 1.5.0

Auto Planner plans several targets at once and merges chains that share resources, picks recipes with up to two of the Planner's strategies (remembered), always merges Modular Miner outputs; Add a node gets data-driven categories with icons and a grouped list (switchable in Settings), a compact details card that grows the panel with buildings, power, per-cycle amounts, fluids in m³, unlocks, a wiki link and Use recipe in its header, compact equal-height tiles and rows with the building icon, a search bar with / and Ctrl+F, filter chips with removable active filters, double-click to add, favorites and search prefixes; connections get a right-click menu and select on click; home page with Datapacks, Your Models and Public Models tabs, a file-manager library and a model details pane; device settings from the home page; older desktop builds on the download page; window size remembered; whole-machine rounding removed; even split behind exact values.

[Read the 1.5.0 patch notes](/production-modeler/changelog/1.5.0/).
