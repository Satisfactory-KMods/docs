## NEW FEATURES

- **Calculations in number fields.** Type `2*10` for 20, or use `+`, `-`, `/`
  and parentheses. Exact; only the result is kept.
  - Rates, limits, counts, clock, multipliers, shares and settings fields.
  - Enter or leaving the field applies it. An unfinished `2*` keeps the old value.
- **At least limits.** A port limit can now be a minimum as well as a ceiling or
  an exact rate. Pick **At least** in the Inspector.
  - Above the minimum the port runs like Auto, up to the other limits. With
    nothing else to size the plan it settles at the minimum.
  - A minimum the connected group cannot reach shows in Messages with the rate
    it can reach.
  - The port icon toggles between the exact rate and the chosen bound. Manual
    mode solves every limit as an exact rate.
- **Connection shares.** A port with more than one connection takes a percent
  per connection in the Inspector. Empty is Auto.
  - The connection label shows the share.
  - Over 100%, or every share set without reaching 100%, shows in Messages.
  - Native files keep shares; legacy SFMD export reports that it cannot.
- **Interaction settings.** New **Interaction** section in Settings.
  - Mouse wheel zooms or pans; drag threshold.
  - Selection box picks touched or enclosed cards.
  - Double-click and right-click on empty canvas.
  - View a model opens on; number of undo steps.
  - Shortcuts dialog marks single-key shortcuts that are switched off.

## QUALITY OF LIFE

- **Calculation modes.** New models offer Manual and Full. A model already in
  None or Basic keeps its mode and still offers it in Settings.
- **Compare page.** Old compare links open the model. Compare game-data
  versions with **Migration** in the toolbar.
- **Clock in percent.** The clock slider, its presets and the machine's range
  read in percent, like the node card. The model still stores the multiplier.
- **Room between planned columns.** The Auto Planner leaves at least 300 px
  between the columns of a chain it places, so its connections stay readable.

## BUGFIXES

- **Byproducts first.** Where routes for one material rejoin, co-products
  already made are used before another recipe makes the same material. Then
  the least raw input wins.
  - Heavy Oil Residue from Distilled Natural Gas is used before Diluted Bitumen
    makes more.
- **Recycling loops.** A machine in a loop with an unused co-product no longer
  runs at a full building and drags the loop up with it. Sized by demand.
- **No machine left at 0.** A machine no longer stands at 0 while its input is
  there, as Petroleum Coke did on Heavy Oil Residue. The coke chain now plans
  without waste.
  - Identical machines on one supply run alike, in Basic and Full.
  - A dead end beside an exact, at most or at least chain takes what the chain
    leaves of the extractor.
  - Basic shares a split evenly in any connection order. In Full the connection
    you moved first takes what it can, except between dead ends: what a dead
    end takes goes nowhere, so they share evenly in Full too.
- **Merges take turns.** Where a demand fixes what a machine takes, its incoming
  connections deliver in turn, like a merger, even when one of them draws more
  from outside. No input stands at 0 while another supplies everything. Least
  waste still comes first.
  - In Full, a machine whose input order you changed follows that order.
  - Where one such merge feeds another, the one nearer the demand takes its
    turns first; the other can still leave an input at 0.
  - An extractor that also feeds a dead end runs down to its turn rather than
    pushing the rest into the dead end.
  - In a loop, what the loop returns still comes first; fresh supply may stay
    at 0 there.
  - A pack and unpack loop that now runs may show that it needs one put in by
    hand to start.
- **Recipes with no input connected.** Such a recipe feeding a merge runs one
  building, like an extractor drawn at 1, instead of standing at 0.
- **Drawn extractor counts.** A count far above what an exact plan needs shrinks
  to the need. A count that only feeds dead ends keeps what you drew and shares
  it between them.
- **Sinks that size the chain.** An at most Sink that sizes its chain is built
  in full, and an at least Sink settles at its minimum. On what a loop makes, an
  at most Sink sizes the loop; on a by-product it only caps it.
- **Returned material first.** What a loop returns is used before fresh supply,
  whatever order the connections were made in. Full follows your connection
  order only among plans that waste equally little.
- **Saved view.** Zoom buttons, minimap and double-click zoom after a plain
  click on the canvas are remembered again.
- **Decimals, not fractions.** Messages and the Auto Planner's rates show
  decimals instead of exact fractions such as `1/100`.
- **Slow sign-in service.** The app opens signed out after five seconds when the
  sign-in service does not answer, and signs in once it does.
- **Blueprint ids.** Saving a blueprint under an id another account already
  uses is refused instead of creating a second copy.

## GAME DATA

- **Bundled packs.** Vanilla and SatisfactoryPlus exported with KModelerExporter
  1.5.0.
  - SatisfactoryPlus: the three native Seed Extractor recipes.
  - Power shard types and sort rule counts come from the export. Older packs
    still derive them.
- **Unverified exporter data.** A machine whose clock range the exporter could
  not read keeps the exporter's reason. Plot recipes with unknown slot needs
  stay unverified instead of falling back to class data.
