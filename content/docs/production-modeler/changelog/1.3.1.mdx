## Changed

- List hand-mined resource deposits in their own Deposits section on the wiki
  map, apart from the resource nodes a miner can sit on. Deposits keep their
  own on/off state per resource and are shown by default.
- Show the underground caves and entrances on the wiki map by default. The
  Underground section still turns them off.

## Fixed

- Show the blue, yellow and purple power-slug icons on the desktop wiki map.
  The markers and the World objects legend now use the slug item images from
  the active game-data pack.
- Size dead-end auto buildings to the supply that actually reaches them. A
  recipe with no consumer behind an auto-count miner, or behind a storage that
  passes several producers through, now scales to take everything upstream
  delivers instead of stopping at one building and reporting a surplus.
- Set an exact rate or an upper limit on a merged Modular Miner output without
  breaking the plan. The port keeps its rate, the miner count follows the pin,
  and returning the port to automatic clears the pin.
- Share models from the dev site and dev desktop channel with dev.k-mods.com
  links instead of production links.
