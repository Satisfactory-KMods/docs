## Quality of life

- Desktop `.kdata` import/export without size, file-count or image budgets.
- Browser `.kdata` upload 64 → 128 MiB.
- Separate desktop/browser import hints; message when checked data could not
  be saved.

## Bug fixes

- Faster solves on large chains: redundant constraints removed, exact rates
  and budget unchanged.
- Windows: game data imports/activates after preview; temp files closed before
  replacing registry, recovery state or `.kdata` export.
- KModelerExporter: archive/icon budgets removed (missing pictures in large
  modpacks). Re-export to pick them up.
