Hi Pioneers! The wiki moved into the desktop app, ports take a rate straight
on the canvas, models get icons and colours, the grid snaps everything, and
Refined Power's big machines (Solar Towers, the Icarus receiver, Modular
Reactors) and the burner manufacturers can finally be planned properly.

## NEW FEATURES

- **The wiki, offline.** Browse and search the wiki inside the desktop app
  using records, images and translations from the selected game-data pack.
- **The map, offline.** View the wiki map on desktop with bundled tiles for
  zoom levels 3 to 5 and load savegame overlays locally. Higher zoom levels
  still need an internet connection.
- **Rates on the canvas.** Click a port's rate to set an exact rate, apply an
  upper limit, or hand it back to the automatic calculation. Enter decimals
  or fractions; fluid rates show in cubic metres per minute.
- **Onboarding for new hires.** A three-step getting-started guide on an empty
  canvas or in Help: add buildings, connect ports, set a target rate.
- **One model library.** A model saved on this device and in the cloud is one
  card that says where it lives. File models into folders with drag and drop
  or the card menu, use `/` for subfolders, narrow the list by folder, tag
  chips or storage location, and keep folders and tags on models that are only
  on this device; they travel with the first cloud save.
- **Model icons and colours.** Pick an Iconify icon or a game image, choose a
  colour preset or set your own. The library cards and the editor header carry
  both.
- **Icons without uplink.** Browse and enter MDI icons without an internet
  connection, and reuse existing tags and folders straight from the
  model-details dialog.
- **The grid.** Show the canvas grid with a toggle next to Fit in the
  bottom-left corner. It scales with the zoom, sits on the site background and
  is the same grid buildings snap to; its default cell is 50.
- **Labels snap too.** Dragged connection labels snap to the grid by their
  centre; on 2D routes a label also levels with a nearby port so the line into
  it runs straight.
- **Ctrl overrides snapping.** Hold Ctrl while dragging a building, a label or
  a waypoint to invert snapping for that drag alone.
- **Storage output reads its input.** An output with no part, rate or
  connection shows the input part at the incoming rate. Wiring it on hands
  that part to the belt.
- **Your buildings on the map.** Buildings from a loaded savegame show on the
  wiki map with per-type toggles, search and their real pickup icons.
  Footprints and spline-based structures are drawn as geometry instead of
  marker clouds.
- **Caves and slugs.** Toggle the bundled underground caves and entrances.
  World objects sit in compact category grids with section-level select and
  clear actions, and power slugs show their item icons.
- **Deposits stay.** Resource deposits keep their bound resource, remaining
  amount and hand-mining yield on the map.
- **Home-page hero, restored.** The original wrapping version, update controls
  and build information are back in the hero, and the complete slim
  update/version bar carries into the editor.
- **Burner manufacturers.** Model BurnerManufacturer recipes with a separate,
  selectable fuel input, including clock and Somersloop power scaling without
  electric grid demand.
- **Solar Towers.** Plan Refined Power Solar Towers with the selected fluid
  recipe, linked and visible mirrors, and a daylight snapshot. Installed
  mirrors count in building, construction and power totals even when they
  cannot see the tower.
- **The Icarus receiver.** Plan the single Icarus receiver with daytime,
  cooldown and orbital-stock settings. Global orbital upkeep stays independent
  of receiver duty and the selected Railgun's power counts separately. Launch
  batches and tick-dependent charging times are not simulated.
- **Modular Reactors.** Plan Refined Power Modular Reactors as their complete
  physical assembly: ten chambers, I/O ring, optional cooling addon, selected
  shielding, fuel, cooling medium, heat, clock, tick rate, steam and power
  output.

## QUALITY OF LIFE

- **Model cards, redesigned.** Storage location, folder, tags, an in-place
  editor with tag suggestions, and a right-click menu that moves a model to a
  folder, removes only the local copy, or deletes only the cloud copy.
- **`.kdata` v2.** Accept v2 packs with optional wiki data while continuing to
  read v1 packs. The wiki section survives a desktop pack export.
- **SatisfactoryPlus, bundled.** SatisfactoryPlus game data ships as a bundled
  `.kdata` archive alongside Vanilla, in the same pack format as imported game
  data.
- **Bigger uploads.** The browser `.kdata` upload limit grows from 128 MiB to
  512 MiB and packs may hold up to 10,000 files.
- **Less redrawing.** Selecting or moving nodes in large models triggers fewer
  redraws and summary calculations, and changing summary settings no longer
  restarts the production calculation.
- **Pin the count in the footer.** Pin a building count directly in the footer
  chip that shows it: the solved count is the placeholder, typing a number
  fixes it, clearing hands it back to the calculation. The separate field in
  the card header is gone.
- **Card names on one line.** Long names are cut with an ellipsis; the tooltip
  carries the full name. Cards are 50 px wider and the footer chips share one
  height.
- **One dialog anatomy.** Every dialog, in the Modeler and across the site,
  shares eyebrow, title, description, content and footer, including Escape and
  backdrop handling.
- **Outposts and blueprints as sub-factories.** The container's ports are the
  same on both sides. What you connect to an input on the overview is handed
  out inside under "From outside", so it can feed the machines there; what the
  machines deliver under "To outside" leaves through the container's output.
  Models wired the earlier way keep working.
- **Model details from the header.** Click the model's icon, colour and name
  in the editor header to open its details; the redundant edit and details
  buttons beside it are gone.
- **Decimals in tooltips.** Tooltip amounts show as numbers with four decimals
  instead of fractions such as "9549/80/min". Installs that kept the earlier
  defaults move along; a format you chose yourself stays as it is.

## BUGFIXES

- Preserve a saved canvas position and zoom when opening an imported model.
- Save model settings after changing recipe input, power, or Space Elevator
  multipliers.
- Switch the language while reading the wiki and see the page in that language
  right away, instead of after the next reload.
- Let outposts without a connected supply represent external production sized
  to downstream demand. Keep explicit rate limits and balance inputs against
  outputs when a supply is connected.
- Load imported `.kdata` packs again. A pack whose parts lacked fields the
  schema fills in failed its integrity check on every open with "Required game
  data is missing", although the import itself had succeeded.
- Stop the "Recover local draft" dialog from returning on every visit after
  restoring a draft: the restored draft continues the cloud copy, so its next
  save is accepted instead of being rejected as stale.
- Keep a draft's creation date and its backup marker across saves, and show the
  "Saved" toast once when settings are saved.
- Open the app's home page when clicking the logo in the desktop app instead of
  leaving for k-mods.com.
- Preserve planned Icarus stocks and existing port identities when explicitly
  mapping renamed orbital items during a game-data migration.
- Save a game image chosen as a model icon to the cloud without a 422
  validation error. Creation, metadata edits and local validation accept the
  same formats.
- Keep draft recovery actions disabled while the model is loading or another
  operation is running, so an early restore click is not silently rejected.

## GAME DATA

The bundled Vanilla and SatisfactoryPlus packs currently contain catalog data
only. To use the desktop wiki, import a `.kdata` pack that includes wiki data.
Existing v1 packs still work for modeling. Modeler 1.2.1 and older cannot
import v2 packs.

BurnerManufacturer, Solar Tower, Icarus and Modular Reactor support needs fresh
game data from the updated KModelerExporter. Missing or inconsistent
special-building metadata produces diagnostics instead of a supported
calculation.
