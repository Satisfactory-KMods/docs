Most buildings are an ordinary recipe: fixed inputs, fixed outputs, one machine.
Satisfactory Plus adds several that are not, and each one is modelled from what
the game data actually says.

## Refined Power

A boiler record names every building that has to cooperate, so a boiler and its
heater both become required roles on one node. You cannot place half an assembly.
Coolers and turbines stand alone.

The material flow is complete. The electricity is not: a turbine only spins, and
the generator attached to it derives its output from an RPM curve that the data
export does not contain. A turbine node therefore says that its power is not
modelled instead of quietly reporting zero.

## Slug Hatchery and Terrarium

Both produce only while they are comfortable. Temperature and humidity have to
sit inside the ranges of the slug you are hatching or breeding, and outside them
the building makes nothing at all. The environment gates production, it never
scales it, so inside the range the exported rates apply unchanged.

Without modules a hatchery sits at 25 degrees and 0.5 humidity, which is where a
new node starts.

:::caution[Expected value is a lower bound]
Some slug outcomes raise their own chance after every failed cycle. The exported
chance is only the starting probability, so an expected value computed from it
understates what you actually get. The modeler flags exactly which outputs this
affects rather than presenting a number that looks precise.
:::

## Farming plots

A plot offers one slot per module type: harvester, irrigation and greenhouse. A
recipe that requires a slot will not solve without it.

Only the greenhouse changes the rate. It runs the plot at 1.5 times its
potential, which speeds up the whole cycle, so a greenhouse plot consumes half
again as much seed and water as well as producing half again as much crop.

## Air Collector

An Air Collector produces from what it can reach. You set how many nodes or
pillars it scans and how many other collectors sit nearby, and the rate follows:
the scan count times the yield per hit, clamped to the record's own minimum and
maximum, divided by the crowding penalty, rounded down to steps of 500.

An unscanned collector still produces the minimum.

One variant works from build height instead of scanning. There you enter how
high the collector stands, and its base output scales from once to five times
across the range the buildable allows.

## Modular Miner

A modular miner is assembled from a head, optional boosters, and a waste or
fluid module. Which combinations are allowed, what they cost in power, and how
they change the extraction rate all come from the miner description for that
resource.

## Solar panels and wind turbines

These follow the world rather than a fuel, so they have no input to size them.
Instead the node carries a count, and the summary shows the range the export
describes: a solar panel between its night and day output, a wind turbine
between nothing and its ceiling.

There is no long run average. A solar panel's day and night mix is not exported,
and wind power scales an unexported height curve by the current wind. The modeler
reports the bounds and says that the average is unknown.
