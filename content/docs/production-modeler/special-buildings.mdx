The palette offers routing, storage, extractors, generators and special
assemblies. Available buildings and settings depend on your model's game data.

- **Open palette:** double-click or right-click empty canvas. **A** or **Insert**
  opens it near the view centre. There is no **+ Add** toolbar button.
- **Add and connect:** drag a port connection into empty space, then choose a
  compatible node.
- **Find an entry:** search names, inputs or outputs. Switch between grid and
  detailed list. Recipe filters cover mods, tiers, progression and alternates.
  Search and filters reset when the palette reopens.

| Special node | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Outpost / Blueprint** | Group nodes behind boundary ports. Double-click to enter. Not individual game buildings. |
| **Splitter** | One solid part. Starts with one input and three outputs. |
| **Merger** | Combine the same solid part. Starts with three inputs and one output. |
| **Pipeline Junction** | One liquid or gas. Starts with two inputs and two outputs. No solids. |
| **AWESOME Sink** | Consume sinkable solids and report points per minute. No fluids or unsinkable items. |
| **Storage / Fluid Buffer** | Store solids or fluids. Choose the building and state in the inspector. |
| **Dimensional Depot** | Upload eligible solids with an optional upload-tier ceiling. |
| **Space Elevator** | Delivery endpoint. Phase costs and rates are unavailable, so delivery calculation is unsupported. |

Splitters, mergers and junctions carry **one part per node**. Add or remove ports
in the inspector. Splitting does not create different products; merging cannot
mix Iron and Copper Ingots. In **Full** mode, connection order sets priority on
a shared port. Older priority nodes still work but are no longer palette entries.

**Storage states:**

- **Partially full** (default): incoming and outgoing rates may differ.
- **Empty:** blocks outgoing flow.
- **Full:** blocks incoming flow.
- **Input equals output:** balances each part's incoming and outgoing rates.

Storage does not simulate filling time or stock depletion.

**Extractors:** set resource, available purity, node count and clock in the
inspector. Automatic calculation may grow an unfixed count to meet demand.
**Keep this count fixed** prevents growth. In automatic modes this is a capacity
ceiling, not a fixed output rate. Clock bounds depend on the building.

**Fuel generators:** choose fuel and clock. Supply the required fuel and
supplemental resources; handle any waste ports. Only supported generator
profiles appear in the palette.

## Burner manufacturers

Recipes made in a machine based on **BurnerManufacturer** have a separate fuel
input. Choose **Fuel** in the inspector and supply both that input and the recipe
ingredients. If the recipe also uses the selected fuel as an ingredient, keep
the two inputs connected separately.

Fuel use follows the machine's producing power, clock and Somersloop settings.
It does not add electric grid consumption. Loading several fuel items at once
changes the in-game inventory requirement, not the average fuel rate. The
modeler calculates continuous operation, not fuel-loading delays or startup.

Changing fuel removes incompatible fuel connections while keeping recipe
connections. Switching to an electric producer removes the fuel input. Both
changes can be undone. Import fresh game data from KModelerExporter if your
catalog does not contain the machine's burner profile. Missing fuel or power
data prevents a supported calculation.

## Refined Power

- **Boiler:** includes its required heater on the same assembly node. Neither
  role can be omitted. Coolers have separate assemblies.
- **Turbine:** select a compatible **Generator** role when available. Power uses
  that generator's curve and ceiling at the turbine's maximum running RPM.
- **Assumptions:** steady running, session power multiplier of 1. Startup and
  shutdown are not simulated.
- **Missing power data:** shows a warning, not a reliable zero-power result.
  Material flow can still calculate when RPM, generator curve or ceiling data
  is missing.

## Solar Tower

Choose a Solar Tower assembly for its input fluid, then select a compatible
mirror. Set **Linked mirrors**, **Mirrors with line of sight**, and **Daylight
factor** in the inspector. These describe a snapshot, not average production
over a full day. Use 0 for night and the game's daylight factor between 0 and 1
otherwise. New nodes start with enough clear mirrors at factor 1.

Only mirrors with line of sight contribute heat. The tower clamps their combined
heat between 29 and 600, then checks the selected fluid's minimum heat. Too
little heat prevents calculation; extra heat does not speed up the recipe.
Its water/fluid consumption, output and cycle time come from the active modpack.
Installed mirrors count toward buildings, construction cost and power even
when they are obscured. Environment changes support undo/redo.

Export fresh game data to include these profiles. The exporter uses the first
matching recipe in RefinedRDApi's live list, just as the tower does. Missing
runtime recipes, cycle data or mirror ratings produce explicit diagnostics.

## Icarus

Add an **Icarus** assembly and select a compatible Railgun. The receiver's water
and steam amounts, cycle time, cooldown and orbital stock thresholds come from
your modpack. Set **Daytime**, **Cooldown active** and each **Orbital stock** in
the inspector. These are planned operating conditions, not a live save reading.
Night, an active cooldown or insufficient orbital stock prevents calculation.

The game permits one receiver per world. Its duty can fall below 100 percent,
but the model cannot create another receiver to meet excess demand or multiply
it through an Outpost/Blueprint count. Construction cost still includes one
whole receiver and one selected Railgun at partial duty.

Orbital upkeep is separate from the water/steam cycle: supply **3 of every
orbital item per minute**, even when receiver demand is lower or zero. The real
subsystem also drains stocks at night. Keep enough stock above the displayed
minimum to bridge the gaps between launches.

Railgun charge capacity, accepted items and the 26-second firing sequence are
exported. Charging itself advances per game tick, so this plan does **not**
predict charging times, launch batches or how many Railguns sustain a particular
batch schedule. The selected Railgun's operating power is counted independently
of receiver duty. Check the actual launch schedule in game; the material rates
describe sustained operation with adequately maintained orbital stocks.

## Slug Hatchery and Terrarium

- **Environment:** set temperature and humidity in the inspector. In game,
  out-of-range conditions stop production rather than scale its speed.
- **New nodes:** start within the slug's comfort ranges. The module-free values
  of 25 degrees and 0.5 humidity are kept where suitable; otherwise the range
  midpoint is used. Maintain these conditions with modules in game.
- **Comfort warnings:** planning continues at active recipe rates. Saved
  readings stay unchanged. **Use comfortable conditions** resets them and can
  be undone. Invalid or unsupported readings cause calculation errors.
- **Time of day:** hatchery rates cover active time only. The day/night gate is
  not modelled. Terrarium breeding has no time-of-day check.
- **Use expected value:** requests chance-weighted output, not guaranteed slugs
  per egg. Chance-bearing recipes and the model's chance setting can also
  enable this.

:::caution[Expected value is a lower bound]
Some outcomes become more likely after failed cycles. The modeler uses the
corrected long-run share where supported. Outputs with an approximation warning
use the starting chance as a lower bound instead. Check warnings before relying
on a slug production target.
:::

## Farming plots

Choose the crop's plot entry, then edit **Modules**.

- One slot each: harvester, irrigation and greenhouse.
- Required modules start selected. Missing, unsupported or forbidden module
  combinations cause calculation errors.
- Greenhouse: **50 percent faster cycles**. Both seed/water consumption and crop
  output rise by 50 percent. Other modules do not change the rate.

## Air Collector

Set **Environment** in the inspector. Canvas placement does not supply these
readings.

- **Scanning variant:** enter scanned nodes or pillars and other collectors in
  range. An unscanned collector still produces the minimum.
- **Height variant:** enter **Build height** in centimetres. The base multiplier
  rises from 1 at 4,000 cm (40 m) to 5 at 45,000 cm (450 m), clamped at both ends.
  New nodes start at 4,000 cm with no neighbours.
- **Crowding:** divides the amount per cycle by 1 plus 1.1 times the other
  collector count. Both variants apply it.

The scan yield or height yield is clamped to the building's amount limits,
reduced by crowding, rounded down in 500-unit steps, then clamped again. Those
are amount units, not per-minute rates. Cycle duration determines the final rate.

One variant works from build height instead of scanning. There you enter how
high the collector stands, and its base output scales from once to five times
across the range the buildable allows. Height is measured in centimetres and
can include a fractional part. For example, 8,817.5 cm with one nearby collector
yields 3,500 litres per six-second cycle, or 35 cubic metres per minute, for the
height-based air profile.

## Modular Miner

Choose the resource's palette entry. Configure it in the inspector:

- **Mining head:** meet the displayed minimum head requirement.
- **Waste attachment / Fluid attachment:** choose compatible options. The
  configuration determines output, waste and fluid-input ports.
- **Booster slots:** choose boosters individually. Some resources require them.
  Available slots, rate bonuses and power costs depend on the resource.
- **Merge both outputs onto one belt:** combines the two main outputs only when
  both contain the same part. It does not mix resources or convert waste.
- **Changing attachments:** disconnect a fixed input before removing it through
  a configuration change.

Clock scales material rates, including acid intake. Fixed fluid intake is per
installed miner, so partial production can still require the full intake at the
chosen clock. Check the clock control's range: a Modular Miner can stop at
100 percent where an ordinary miner supports 250 percent.

## Dimensional Depot

Connect eligible solids, then select **Depot upload tier** in the inspector.

- **Rates:** 15 items/min at base. Standard research adds 30, 60, 120 and
  240 items/min tiers where present in the game data.
- **Tier ceiling:** caps total incoming flow across all depot inputs. It does
  not demand that rate or detect your save's completed research.
- **No tier:** a new depot, a cleared tier or missing tier data leaves upload
  uncapped. Calculation continues with a warning.
- **Custom ceiling:** set an input port to **At most**. This limits that port
  only, not the whole depot.

## Solar panels and wind turbines

- **Solar panels:** set **Node count** in the inspector. Power uses the exported
  night/day range multiplied by count. No long-run average is calculated, and
  the day value is not guaranteed constant output.
- **Wind turbines:** not offered as supported generators. Missing session,
  height and wind data prevent a supported power calculation, including for
  imported turbines. Nearby-turbine placement restrictions are not simulated.

## Building limits

Capacity and fixed rates are different controls:

- **At most 60/min:** a ceiling. Actual flow may be lower.
- **Exactly 60/min:** a fixed rate. Can conflict with supply or another limit.
- **Switch port mode:** use the inspector or click the node's existing limit
  badge. Recipe machine limits also offer **At most** and **Exactly**.
- **Capacity:** on recipes and sinks, the selected belt or pipe caps total
  connected flow in each direction. It is not a production target. Depots use
  their upload tier instead.
- **Manual mode:** entered machine and port limits become exact constraints.

**Save-wide building limits** are separate. The machines summary shows the
model's usage, remaining allowance and excess. It does not count buildings
already placed in your save. Exceeding the allowance warns without blocking
calculation. Missing assembly roles, invalid modules and incompatible parts can
still block the affected calculation.

## FaxIT Network

Choose **FaxIT Network** when drive profiles are available, then select
**FaxIT drives** in the inspector. This is a network, not a drive-crafting recipe.

- Drives contribute power draw and capacity for distinct item types.
- No drives, too many copies of a drive, or too many distinct parts cause errors.
- Only parts marked as storable in FaxIT are accepted.
- Storage duration and filling time are not simulated.
